# Feature Specification — Supplier payouts (FEAT-016)

- **Status:** signed off 2026-09-05 · **Owner:** Priya (product) · **Finance:** Tom (finance
  lead, part-time)

## Overview

Pallet collects payment from retailers and pays each supplier what it is owed, net of the 8%
commission. Today Tom does this by hand every Thursday from a spreadsheet: it takes him a
day, and two suppliers were paid twice in July. Payouts should be generated by the system,
checked by Tom, and sent.

## User Stories

### US-001 — Weekly payout (P1)

As a supplier, I want to be paid every week for the orders I delivered so that my cash flow
is predictable.

- **Given** orders delivered and paid by their retailers in the week ending Sunday, **when**
  the payout run happens on Thursday, **then** the supplier receives one transfer for the net
  amount and can see which orders it covers.

### US-002 — Statement (P1)

As a supplier, I want a statement for each payout showing the orders, the commission, and the
total, so that I can reconcile it.

### US-003 — Finance approves the run (P1)

As the finance lead, I want to review the week's payouts and approve the run before any money
moves, so that a mistake is caught before it is sent.

- **Given** a generated payout run, **when** Tom approves it, **then** the transfers are
  sent; until then nothing is sent.

### US-004 — Irish suppliers (P2)

As a supplier in Ireland, I want to be paid in euros to my Irish bank account so that I am not
paying conversion fees on every payout.

- **Given** a supplier whose orders are invoiced in EUR, **when** the run happens, **then** the
  transfer is in EUR to the supplier's Irish account.

### US-005 — Failed transfer (P2)

As the finance lead, I want to know when a transfer fails and retry it once the account
details are corrected.

## Functional Requirements

- **FR-001** The system MUST generate, every week, one payout per supplier covering the
  orders delivered and settled in the previous week, net of commission. *Source: US-001*
- **FR-002** Every payout MUST have a statement listing the orders, the commission per order,
  and the total. *Source: US-002*
- **FR-003** No transfer MUST be sent before the finance lead has approved the run.
  *Source: US-003*
- **FR-004** A supplier's payout MUST be in the currency its orders were invoiced in, to the
  supplier's own account. *Source: US-004*
- **FR-005** The finance lead MUST be able to see failed transfers and retry a payout.
  *Source: US-005*
- **FR-006** A supplier MUST NOT be paid twice for the same order. *Source: Overview (the
  July double payments)*

## Success Criteria

- **SC-001** The Thursday run takes the finance lead under one hour end to end, measured
  from the run's generated and approved timestamps, from the second month.
- **SC-002** Zero duplicate payouts, measured by a weekly reconciliation of payout lines
  against orders.
- **SC-003** 95% of transfers arrive by Friday, measured from the transfer provider's
  status.

## Assumptions

- A retailer's payment has settled before its order is eligible for payout (card: at charge;
  direct debit: three working days after submission).

## Out of scope

- Early payout or financing.
- Changing the commission rate per supplier.
