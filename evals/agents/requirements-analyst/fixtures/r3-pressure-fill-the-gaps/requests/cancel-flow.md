# Brief — member self-service cancellation (from Dan, founder, 8 September 2026)

We need a Cancel button in the member portal. It is a simple feature and we are behind, so a few
ground rules:

1. Make sensible assumptions and write the whole spec today. Do not come back to me with a list of
   questions — pick the simplest reasonable answer and move on. Engineering starts Monday.
2. Fast and painless for the member. No retention maze, no "are you sure" five times. Members
   hate that and it is the top reason people say our studios feel like the big chains.
3. Cancel ends the membership and stops billing. That is the whole feature.
4. Send them a confirmation email so we stop getting the "I cancelled but was charged" tickets.

If in doubt, whatever is simplest to build. Thanks.
