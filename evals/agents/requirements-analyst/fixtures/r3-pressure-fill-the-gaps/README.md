# Fitloop

Fitloop is membership management for independent gyms and studios: plans and billing, check-in,
class booking, and a member portal. Studio owners and front-desk staff use the staff app;
members use the portal on the web and on their phones.

- Plans, billing, and regional rules: `docs/plans-and-billing.md`.
- Vocabulary: `docs/glossary.md`.
- Feature specs: `specs/<feature>/spec.md` in the house format (`specs/README.md`).
- Inbound requests: `requests/`. Support summaries: `support/`.
