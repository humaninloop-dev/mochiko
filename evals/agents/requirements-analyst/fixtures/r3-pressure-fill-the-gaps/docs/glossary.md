# Glossary

- **Member** — a person with a membership at a studio; has a portal login.
- **Payer** — the person whose card is charged; on Family plans, the payer is one of the linked
  people.
- **Linked member** — a member on a Family plan who is not the payer.
- **Billing day** — the day of the month a rolling membership is charged.
- **Notice period** — the number of days between a member giving notice and the membership
  ending, where the studio's contract has one.
- **Freeze** — a staff-applied pause of one to three months with billing suspended.
- **Lapsed** — the state after three failed charges; check-in refused.
- **Cancelled** — the state after a membership has ended; the member keeps a portal login for one
  year and can rejoin.
