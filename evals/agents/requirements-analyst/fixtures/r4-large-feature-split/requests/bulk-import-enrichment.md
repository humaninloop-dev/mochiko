# Enrichment — bulk volunteer import (concluded 5 September 2026)

## Who

Coordinators bringing an existing volunteer base onto Rota — typically 50 to 2,000 people held in
a spreadsheet (Excel or Google Sheets, sometimes exported from a church database or a festival's
sign-up form).

## Problem

Adding volunteers one form at a time takes days for anyone with more than a few dozen names. A
coordinator with 800 names on a spreadsheet gave up after 60 and went back to WhatsApp; two
organisations that signed up in August have not scheduled a single shift because their volunteers
are not in Rota yet.

## Value

An organisation with a spreadsheet is scheduling real shifts within its first week on Rota,
instead of stalling at the door. For us: activation. For the coordinator: their evenings back.

## Interview notes (three coordinators, 1–4 September)

- Spreadsheets vary: column headings are inconsistent ("Email", "E-mail address", "Contact");
  some have first and last name in one column; phone numbers are in the local format with
  spaces; one sheet has a "Left?" column marking people who have moved away.
- Duplicate rows are common — the same person added twice over the years, sometimes with
  different capitalisation of the email.
- All three want to look at what will happen before anything is sent: "I do not want 800 people
  getting an email because I clicked the wrong thing."
- Two asked whether they could import people now and invite them later, in groups, "so the
  first-aiders get in first" and "so I can check the list with the committee first".
- One coordinator has about 3,000 names ("the whole village signed up for the festival"); the
  other two have 800 and 120.
- Everyone asked what happens if they import the wrong file — can they undo it?
- Interview question we did not get an answer to: when a row's email already belongs to a
  volunteer in the organisation, should the import update that volunteer's details from the
  spreadsheet, skip the row, or ask the coordinator? Two coordinators said "update", one said "ask
  me" — and the "update" answers assumed the spreadsheet was newer, which is not always true.

## Not decided (minor, for the spec author)

- Which file types to accept.
- Whether a header row is required or detected.
- What the summary the coordinator receives after an import says.
- How many rows a single file may carry.
