# Glossary

- **Organisation** — a group using Rota; owns its volunteers, shifts, and coordinators.
- **Coordinator** — an organisation's admin; creates shifts, invites volunteers, sends messages.
- **Volunteer** — a person who can be scheduled. Exists as a record from the moment a coordinator
  adds them; becomes an **active** volunteer once they accept their invitation.
- **Invitation** — the email that asks a volunteer to join the organisation on Rota; carries a
  link that is valid for 14 days.
- **Consent** — the volunteer's agreement to receive scheduling messages from the organisation,
  recorded when they accept the invitation.
- **Tag** — a free-text label on a volunteer (first-aider, driver, evenings-only).
- **Availability** — the days and times a volunteer says they can be scheduled.
