# Pilot — Northgate Coffee (founder's note, 2026-07-12)

Northgate Coffee runs three cafés (Northgate, Canal Street, Station) with 38 staff. They are
our reference customer for the next release and want shift cover live for their staff by
**2026-08-24** — six weeks from the shift-cover kick-off — ahead of the autumn term.

What we know from their managers and from the app's usage data:

- About 60 % of their staff are part-time students. On average a staff member opens the app
  **twice a week**, almost always the day the rota is published.
- Cover is arranged on WhatsApp today: 20 to 30 messages per site per week. Roughly two-thirds
  are a manager asking "can anyone do Saturday 2–8?" for a shift nobody is on; the rest are
  staff trying to give a shift away.
- The disputes that reach the owner ("I thought Sam was covering me") all come from the
  staff-to-staff kind; the manager's shift-hunting is just slow, not contested.
- Managers say the single thing that would change their week is not having to chase people.

They are aware Xero payroll is not built yet and are fine with the CSV.
