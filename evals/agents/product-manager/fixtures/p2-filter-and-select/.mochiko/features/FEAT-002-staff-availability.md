# FEAT-002 — Staff availability

> Status: delivered
> since 2026-04-02 · sticky — live rows may still be visible below

## Capability

Staff declare when they can work — a recurring weekly pattern plus one-off exceptions — and
the rota draft flags any shift that falls outside it.

## Extent

- Recurring availability by weekday and time band; one-off unavailability by date.
- A shift outside availability is flagged on the draft; the manager can still place it.
- Not: staff blocking a specific shift after publish.

## Relations

- composes-with: FEAT-001 — conflicts are surfaced on the rota draft
