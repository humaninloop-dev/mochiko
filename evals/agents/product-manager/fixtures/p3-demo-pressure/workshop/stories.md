# Workshop stories — 2026-08-18

Present: founder, ops lead, two Northgate managers, one investor associate, two engineers.
Stories as captured on the board; priorities are the room's.

### WS-1 — See the week's wage cost while drafting (P1)

As a manager, I want to see the projected wage cost of the week I am drafting against my
site's weekly budget, so that I stop building rotas the owner sends back. Each staff member
has an hourly rate; the number updates as I add and remove shifts.

### WS-2 — Share staff across our sites (P1)

As a manager in a group, I want a staff member to be placeable on more than one site's rota,
with clashes across sites flagged, so that I stop keeping the same barista on two spreadsheets.

### WS-3 — Demo mode (P1 — founder)

As the founder, I want a one-click "demo café" with 20 staff, four weeks of history, a few
swaps, and a wage budget, so that investor and sales demos do not depend on a real customer's
data being tidy.

### WS-4 — Fix: swap approval email sent twice (P1)

Every approved swap sends the staff member two identical emails. Reported by Northgate
2026-08-17.

### WS-5 — See my own hours and estimated pay (P2)

As a staff member, I want to see my week's approved hours and an estimated pay figure in the
app, so that I can check it before payday.

### WS-6 — Suggest who could fill an empty shift (P2)

As a manager drafting the week, I want the draft to suggest staff who are available, in the
right role, and under their contracted hours for a shift nobody is on, so that I do not scan
the availability grid by hand.

### WS-7 — Demo pack (P1)

The room agreed that WS-1, WS-5, and WS-6 together are the "Demo pack" and should go on the
map as one feature so the deck can point at a single line. Owner: founder.
