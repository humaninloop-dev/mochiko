# Stress-test review — spec `lunch-orders`

Reviewer: independent stress-test seat (mochiko:review-specifications). Authored none of the
reviewed material. This is gap-finding input for the run's lead — not a clearing PASS/FAIL
verdict.

**Coverage:** `spec.md` (Intent, Overview, User Stories, Edge Cases, FRs, Key Entities, Success
Criteria, Screens & Flows, Feature Selection, Assumptions, Open Questions, Technical notes),
all four story files (`US-1`..`US-4`), `derivation.md`, `prototype/` (`index.html` + 4 screen
files), plus baseline context: `CLAUDE.md`, `README.md`, `docs/free-school-meals.md`,
`docs/decisions/0003-order-cutoff.md`, `FEATURES.md`, all four `FEAT-XXX` entries,
`.mochiko/specs/index.md`, and the sibling in-flight spec `menu-admin/` (touched by FEAT-002,
which `lunch-orders` also depends on).

**Prototype method:** no server could be started this session. The prototype's own README
states it needs none ("open `index.html` directly; nothing to build or serve"). I walked it by
reading every screen file and tracing every `href` by hand — every link, dead end, and
manifest/file mismatch below was confirmed this way rather than by an interactive click.

## Clarifications

1. **Free school meals in the ordering flow.** The Overview and Assumptions currently say every
   order is charged to a wallet with a card on file — but per `docs/free-school-meals.md`, ~59%
   of pupils at the pilot schools get a funded meal and many families have no card at all.
   - **Option A** — the order flow checks the child's funded status (already tracked per
     FEAT-001's family-accounts extent) and charges £0 / skips the wallet check for a funded
     meal, same as FEAT-003 already does for top-ups.
   - **Option B** — funded children are entirely out of scope for this spec and continue on
     paper slips until a follow-up spec covers them.
   - **Option C** — something else the schools lead has already decided that isn't written down
     yet.
   Impact: Option B means the pilot schools can't actually retire paper slips for most pupils
   this term, which contradicts SC-004 ("paper slips fall to zero... within one term").

2. **Which cut-off is real: Thursday 17:00 or Sunday midnight?** FR-005 and the ratified decision
   say Thursday 17:00 the week before; US-1's own acceptance scenario assumes Sunday midnight.
   - **Option A** — Thursday 17:00 stands as decided; US-1's scenario is a drafting mistake and
     needs correcting.
   - **Option B** — the cut-off decision is being revisited and 0003 needs a new decision entry.
   Impact: whichever is wrong will pass its own independent test while shipping a cut-off the
   kitchen didn't agree to.

3. **What does the kitchen's Sheet write actually contain?** FR-006 commits to writing orders
   into the shared Google Sheet but not which fields.
   - **Option A** — aggregate counts and allergy flags only (mirrors what the governance
     principle says the kitchen role may see: "per-day counts and allergy flags by first name
     and class, never the full record").
   - **Option B** — the full order row (child name, class, meal, allergy detail) since the sheet
     is the interim "kitchen counts" view until FEAT-004 exists.
   Impact: Option B means Confidential pupil data reaches a spreadsheet outside the API
   allowlist/contract-suite enforcement the governance principle names as its control.

4. **Is meal rating actually wanted for this release?** FR-009 traces to no story, and it's the
   only FR in the table without a source.
   - **Option A** — drop it from this spec's scope; it can surface as its own idea later with a
     story behind it.
   - **Option B** — it's wanted now; a story and acceptance scenario need writing so it's
     testable and belongs in FEAT-005's (or a split-out feature's) extent honestly.
   Impact: left as-is, a reviewer can't tell whether it's a real commitment or a placeholder,
   and it's inflating FEAT-005 past a one-breath capability.

5. **How many weeks ahead can a parent order?** (Already flagged by the author in Open
   Questions, still unresolved and now blocking — FR-001 says "any published week" with no
   bound, and the "Out of scope: ordering for a whole term at once" line implies a bound exists
   without stating it.)
   - **Option A** — bounded to however many weeks the kitchen actually publishes ahead (likely
     one, per the prototype's single "Week of 14 September" mock).
   - **Option B** — an explicit cap (e.g. current + next published week) independent of how far
     the kitchen happens to publish.
   Impact: without a bound, "for a whole term" and "for the coming week" aren't actually
   distinguished by any FR.

---

## Recommended verdict (advisory — not a clearing gate)

Not ready to build as staged. F1–F4 are each independently blocking: two of them (F1, F2) are
contradictions the spec itself doesn't know it contains, one (F3) contradicts a ratified
decision, and one (F4) means the P1 flow and the safety-critical allergy flow can't be walked or
traced as drawn. None require new scope to fix — they need the free-school-meals fact, the
in-flight FEAT-002 constraint, and the ratified cut-off decision actually threaded through, and
the prototype's dead links and mis-keyed flow corrected against the manifest that already
describes the intended flows correctly in prose.

**Strengths:** the story layer is well-prioritized and each story carries a genuinely
independent test; the allergy-safety mechanism (marked, disabled options) is exactly right where
the prototype implements it (SCR-001), it just isn't wired to the right acceptance scenario yet.
