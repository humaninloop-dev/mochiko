## Clarifications needed

### C1: How does a funded (free-school-meal) child place an order?
**Question**: FEAT-003 already supports zero-cost funded meals, but this spec's FRs, entities, and screens only model a wallet charge. What should the ordering flow do for a funded child?
**Options**: 1. Same flow, but the order confirms at £0 and skips the wallet-sufficiency check entirely for that child · 2. A visibly different "funded — no charge" state on the summary screen so parents aren't confused about wallet balance · 3. Funded status is out of scope for this run and explicitly deferred (naming the gap for schools until then)
**Why it matters**: Up to 59% of pupils at the pilot schools are funded; shipping without this either blocks most orders or silently mischarges families.

### C2: Do parents see hidden menu items or not?
**Question**: This spec's FR-002 says parents must see hidden items; the in-flight menu-admin spec says hidden items must never reach a parent-facing response. Which rule wins?
**Options**: 1. Drop FR-002's hidden-item clause and defer entirely to menu-admin's rule (hidden truly means hidden) · 2. Menu-admin's rule only applies to the general week view; lunch-orders shows hidden items for some other reason (name it) · 3. Reopen menu-admin's FR-002 to add an ordering-specific exception
**Why it matters**: Kitchen hides items for supplier recalls and safety reasons; showing them to parents anyway during ordering could let someone order a recalled or unsafe item.

### C3: What should a parent be able to do about an order already placed?
**Question**: US-2 is P1 but has zero screens or flows in the manifest or prototype. What should the "view/change/cancel an existing order" experience look like?
**Options**: 1. Reuse SCR-001 (week menu) in an "editing" mode showing already-chosen meals with a change/cancel control per day · 2. A separate "My order this week" screen distinct from the ordering flow · 3. Changes only via re-running the whole ordering flow (pick again, confirm again)
**Why it matters**: Without this, a wrong order stays wrong until the cut-off, and parents have no way to act on the safety/cost reason this feature exists.

### C4: What happens when the wallet can't cover the order?
**Question**: US-1 scenario 3 requires "asked to top up, order held until they do," but no screen shows this. What should the parent see at the payment step when the wallet is short?
**Options**: 1. Inline shortfall banner with a top-up amount and a link to top-up, order saved as pending · 2. Redirect straight into the top-up flow, returning to order summary after · 3. Block the whole order until a separate top-up is done first
**Why it matters**: This is one of three named acceptance scenarios for the P1 story and the mock's own example numbers trigger it, so it isn't a rare edge case.

### C5: Where does the published week menu actually come from for ordering?
**Question**: Is the menu parents order from served by the app (FEAT-002, already in-flight) or read from the catering manager's Google Sheet named in this spec's constraints?
**Options**: 1. Always the app (FEAT-002); the Sheet is only where completed orders get written out for the kitchen · 2. The Sheet is the live source parents order from, and the app's menu-publishing feature is superseded or unused here · 3. Both, kept in sync (name the sync mechanism and its failure mode)
**Why it matters**: This decides whether FR-001/002 are buildable against existing work or need a new Sheet-reading integration, and whether the hidden-item conflict in C2 is even reachable in practice.
