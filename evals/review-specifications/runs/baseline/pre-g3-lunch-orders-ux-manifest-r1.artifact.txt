## Clarifications

### 1. Should the week menu a parent sees include items the kitchen has hidden?
**Why this matters:** FR-002 currently requires the opposite of what the in-flight menu-admin
feature guarantees (F1/F2). Hiding exists for recalls and safety pulls; showing a hidden item to a
parent who then orders it is the exact failure menu-admin was built to prevent, and it's a decision
with a live acceptance test (menu-admin's SC-001) already gating deploys.
- **(a)** Parents never see a hidden item anywhere, matching menu-admin's existing contract — drop
  the "including items the kitchen has marked hidden" clause from FR-002.
- **(b)** Parents see hidden items but cannot order them (e.g., greyed with a reason) — a new,
  distinct behavior that would itself need to be proposed as a delta to FEAT-002, not asserted
  unilaterally by this spec.
- **(c)** Something else the schools lead intended by "so that nothing on offer is missed" that
  isn't about hidden items at all (e.g., today's off-menu / sold-out items) — reword FR-002 to say
  what was actually meant.

### 2. How does a family whose meals are free (free-school-meals) place and pay for an order?
**Why this matters:** At the pilot schools, 59% of pupils get some form of funded lunch per
`docs/free-school-meals.md`, many with no card on file at all. The constitution names the funded
path as a first-class alternative to a wallet charge. The current spec's sole payment path (charge
the wallet at confirmation) and its Assumption ("every family has a card on file") would either
block most pupils from ordering or attempt to charge money nobody owes.
- **(a)** In scope for this run: a funded order records the meal and allergy flag at zero cost, no
  wallet debit, no card required — FR-003 gets a funded-meal branch and a new story/scenario.
  Ordering isn't safely usable at the pilot schools without this.
- **(b)** Deliberately out of scope for this run, deferred to a follow-up spec — but say so
  explicitly (it isn't currently named in Out-of-scope), and be clear about what happens for a
  funded family who tries to order today (blocked entirely? shown an error?).
- **(c)** Handled by an existing mechanism this reviewer hasn't seen (e.g., a school-office-only
  manual override) — point to where that's specified.

### 3. What should a parent see when changing or cancelling an order — and after the cut-off?
**Why this matters:** US-2 is P1 and has real money and kitchen-count consequences, but has zero
screens or flows (F4). Without at least one screen, the "cut-off refusal names the cut-off"
requirement (FR-004) can't be checked against an actual UI before build.
- **(a)** Add a screen showing a placed order with per-day cancel/change controls, plus the
  refusal state (e.g., a banner: "Changes for this week closed Thu 17:00").
- **(b)** Reuse SCR-001 (week menu) as the edit surface post-placement rather than a new screen —
  if so, the manifest should say so and the mock should show that state (already-ordered days
  vs. still-editable days looks identical right now).

### 4. Is a single family checkout across multiple children in scope, or is one child per order session correct?
**Why this matters:** The proposed FEAT-005 extent claims "a parent can order for two or more
children in one sitting without paying twice" (F12), but every story, screen, and flow shows
exactly one child per session. Building to the capability text vs. the demonstrated flow are two
different scopes of work.
- **(a)** One child per ordering session is correct — drop the multi-child claim from the capability
  extent.
- **(b)** Multi-child single-checkout is required — needs its own story, a redesigned child-picker
  (multi-select) and order-summary (grouped by child), and its own acceptance scenario.

### 5. Is meal rating actually wanted for this release?
**Why this matters:** FR-009 discloses no source story and isn't mentioned in Overview or
Out-of-scope (F10); it reads like scope that drifted in rather than a decision anyone made.
- **(a)** Not now — remove FR-009 and the rating bullet from the FEAT-005 extent; revisit later
  behind its own story.
- **(b)** Genuinely wanted this run — write the story (who rates, when, what happens with a low
  rating) and its own screen/flow.

## Recommended verdict

**Not ready to build.** Two findings are blocking on their own: the direct contradiction with the
in-flight menu-admin safety behavior (F1/F2), and the unaddressed funded-meal population that the
product's own docs and governance say must exist (F3). Beyond those, the Screens & Flows layer has
enough dead links and uncovered P1 scenarios (F4–F9) that the prototype cannot currently be used to
validate the one flow (ordering) this feature exists to deliver. Recommend a revision pass on
spec.md + derivation.md + the prototype's missing/dead screens before this proceeds past
stress-test.

strengths: The four stories are individually well-sconed with independent tests, and the allergen-blocking mechanic (US-4, SCR-001's struck-through items) is precisely and concretely specified.
