## Clarifications (decisions the PM/stakeholder can make today)

**Q1 — Max pause length (blocks F1).** Should the spec state decision 0004's terms explicitly
(8-week cap, 6-week warning email, 8-week auto-cancel + slot release), or has something changed
since June that supersedes 0004?
- *Option A:* Adopt 0004 as-is — add FR-010/011/012 covering the cap, the warning email, and
  auto-cancel. No new decision needed, just closing the gap between decision and spec.
- *Option B:* 0004 needs revisiting (e.g. summer-holiday demand argues for a longer cap) — that's
  a founder/ops decision to re-open, not something this spec can silently redefine by omission.
- *Impact:* ship without either and pause has no ceiling — the exact slot-hoarding problem 0004
  exists to prevent.

**Q2 — Direct Debit / PayPal during pause (blocks F2).** When a pause is requested after a
Direct Debit collection is already lodged (or a PayPal charge is already queued), what happens?
- *Option A:* Charge lands as normal for that one week (lodging can't be pulled back), customer
  is told this up front at the point of pausing.
- *Option B:* Charge lands, then Bramble issues an automatic refund (3–5 working days per
  payment-methods.md) with a support-console note explaining why.
- *Impact:* leaving this unstated means engineers will each guess differently, and the guess
  most likely to get shipped (assume everyone's on card, like the Overview says) will actually
  charge ~22% of customers for a week they were told they wouldn't be.

**Q3 — Gift box (blocks F3).** Is "send a paused box as a gift" a real, decided feature, or did
it enter the spec by mistake?
- *Option A:* Drop FR-008 — it has no story and contradicts the stated out-of-scope line.
- *Option B:* It's real and there's a decision for it somewhere not yet in `docs/` — point me at
  it and I'll fold it in properly (with its own story) rather than as a bare FR.

## Minor (logged, not blocking)

- Open Questions' second item ("Should we cache the resume date... offline?") is an
  implementation question, not a product one — belongs in design, not the spec.
- Overview's opening sentence is a long run-on; a tightened version for whoever owns the edit
  (not applied here, per the independence note above):
  > "Customers go on holiday, get a glut from the allotment, or run out of freezer space.
  > Today the only option is skipping at most two deliveries in a row — anything longer means
  > cancelling and losing the delivery slot."
- Interaction between an in-flight `swap-items` selection and a pause requested the same week
  isn't addressed (does a staged swap get dropped, or ride out and apply on resume?). Worth a
  line once F1/F2 are settled, not a blocker for sign-off.

## Strengths

The spec correctly caught and rejected the single-week-skip duplicate itself (US-4 / Filter
Rejections) — the derivation discipline worked before this review ever ran; F1/F2/F7 are the real
gaps, not that check.
