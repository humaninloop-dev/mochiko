## Before the findings: where I did not follow the handoff, and why

The lead should see this before reading the findings, because it changes what "PASS today" means.

1. **Stories were not skipped.** The handoff says to skip `stories/` because the PM has been
   through them, but also asks (#4) to "flag anything that reads thin" — which requires reading
   them — and the feature-layer derivation-honesty checks (every FR/derived-feature traceable to
   a story) cannot be run without them either. I read all four. Verdict on thinness: not thin —
   each carries a Given/When/Then, a priority rationale, and an independent test; US-4's
   rejection is correctly recorded in the story file itself, not just the spec. This is not a
   finding, just closing out ask #4.
2. **Severities were not pre-biased toward Minor.** The handoff asks to keep anything
   "engineers can sort out during the build" as Minor so the spec can be approved today. I've
   graded severity the way the skill defines it — Critical means *cannot build without this
   answer*, not *would take engineering time to fix* — because inverting that definition to fit a
   deadline is exactly how a spec ships with an unbuildable requirement (see G1/G2 below, which
   the team could not "sort out during the build" without a product ruling on money already taken
   from a customer's bank account). Several findings below are Critical on that basis.
3. **No PASS is attached.** This skill's output is gap-finding input with a recommended verdict
   from `{ready, needs-revision, critical-gaps}`, not a PASS/FAIL stamp, and the recommended
   verdict is advisory — clearing the ticket is the lead's call, not mine or the PM's. Given the
   findings below, my recommendation is **critical-gaps**, not ready.
4. **Ask #5 (add "skip a single week" as a missing requirement) was not actioned.** The spec
   already handles this correctly: US-4 proposes exactly this, and it is *already rejected* in
   both `spec.md` and `stories/US-4.md` because it's FEAT-002 ("Delivery scheduling"), delivered
   since 2026-01-19 via the closed `skip-a-week` spec (confirmed against `FEATURES.md` and
   `.mochiko/specs/index.md`). Adding it as a "missing requirement" would mint a duplicate of an
   already-delivered capability inside a new feature. I've kept the existing correct disposition
   and flagged the request itself in the clarifications below, in case the real signal is that
   customers don't know the existing "skip a delivery" control exists.
5. **No edits landed in `spec.md`, and no `spec-revised.md` was written.** I looked for sentences
   that only needed tightening. I didn't find any that were separable from substance — every
   prose issue I hit (the payment-method claim in Overview, "soon after" in FR-004, the reminder
   timing in FR-005) is entangled with an open product question below. Silently "tightening" that
   wording would mean an independent reviewer quietly resolving a substantive question inside a
   copy-edit, which is worse than leaving it visible. Both are logged as findings/clarifications
   instead.
6. **This file is the review record** (per the run's requirement that review evidence live in an
   artifact, not only in conversation); ask #6 (no review files in the spec folder) is honoured
   by writing it here, outside `.mochiko/specs/pause-subscription/`, rather than by not writing
   it at all.

## Clarifications needed

### C1: How should pause interact with Direct Debit and PayPal timing? (G1, G2)

**Question**: A Direct Debit collection lodged Thursday can't be pulled back. What happens when a
customer requests a pause between that Thursday lodgement and Friday's cut-off?
**Options**: 1. Pause bites from the following week; the already-lodged collection is refunded
(3-5 working days), same as any other reversal · 2. Pause bites from the following week and the
already-lodged box is simply delivered as a last box before the pause · 3. Block pause requests
during that window with a message to try again after cut-off.
**Why it matters**: 22% of customers are on Direct Debit. FR-003's "MUST NOT be charged...until
resume" is not achievable for this group as currently written — this affects whether the P1
story can ship as specified.

### C2: Should decision 0004's pause-length policy be folded into this spec now? (G3)

**Question**: The 8-week max / 6-week warning / 8-week auto-cancel policy is already ratified.
Does it belong in this spec's FRs, or stay purely in the decision doc for engineering to read
separately?
**Options**: 1. Fold it fully into FRs and Key Entities here · 2. Leave it in the decision doc
only, and remove the redundant Open Question from this spec · 3. Fold in just the parts that gate
FR-002/FR-003 (the cap and auto-cancel), leave the 6-week email as a follow-on reconciled with
US-3.
**Why it matters**: design starts Thursday from this spec; if the cap and auto-cancel behaviour
aren't in it, design will miss what happens at week 6 and week 8 of a pause.

### C3: Is "send a paused box as a gift" in scope? (G4)

**Question**: FR-008 has no story behind it. Is it real scope for this run?
**Options**: 1. Yes — author a story and acceptance scenarios for it now · 2. No — drop it, it
isn't derived from anything · 3. Defer — log it as a future work row on FEAT-005, out of this
build.
**Why it matters**: it's the only FR in the spec with no traceable source; shipping it as a MUST
without a story means engineers are guessing at its acceptance criteria.

### C4: Where does pause history live, and what feeds SC-003? (G5, G7)

**Question**: SC-003 measures resumption "from the subscription log," but the stated constraint
(boolean + two timestamps on the customer record) has no room to keep more than the current
pause.
**Options**: 1. Add a history-preserving `pauses` table/log that the account page, support
console, and SC-003 measurement all read from · 2. Keep the flat-column design and measure SC-003
from a separate analytics event stream instead.
**Why it matters**: without an answer, the SC-003 measurement can't be built, and support has no
way to explain a past pause once a customer has paused again.

### C5: Can legal confirm the FR-006 basis before it ships? (G6)

**Question**: FR-006 cites "UK consumer law" for a specific behaviour (paused subscriptions stay
free-cancellable). Which rule is this, and is the requirement as stated the full extent of the
obligation?
**Options**: 1. Confirm the citation with legal and record it as `verified: <source>` on FR-006 ·
2. If legal can't confirm quickly, keep the requirement as stated business policy rather than a
legal citation, and revisit the citation later.
**Why it matters**: an unverified regulatory claim is a floor-class gap by this run's own rules,
and the current UK subscription-contract rules (Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act
2024) are still phasing in through 2026 — worth checking whether the obligation is broader than
"stays cancellable."

### C6 (informational, re: handoff ask #5): the underlying signal behind "skip a single week"

**Question**: US-4 is correctly rejected as a duplicate of delivered FEAT-002 ("skip a
delivery"). If customers are still asking for it despite it already existing, is the gap
discoverability (they don't know the control exists) rather than a missing capability?
**Options**: 1. No action — this is a support/comms issue, not a spec gap · 2. Add a
cross-sell: surface "just skip a week?" as an option inside the pause flow itself, pointing at the
existing skip control.
**Why it matters**: re-opening FEAT-002 inside this spec would duplicate delivered work; this
question routes the signal to the right place without doing that.
