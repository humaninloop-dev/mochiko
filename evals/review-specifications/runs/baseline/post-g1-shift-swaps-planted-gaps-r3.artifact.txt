## Clarifications

**Q1 — Swap effective point (blocks build, F1).** When does an accepted swap actually change
the published rota?
- **A. On mutual acceptance** (FR-004/US-1's reading) — fastest for staff, but removes the
  manager gate US-3 was written for; drop FR-006 and US-3's "before it lands" framing, or
  demote manager review to after-the-fact (folds into F1/F6's history-only view).
- **B. On manager approval** (FR-006/US-3's reading) — matches the licensing-sensitive
  rationale in US-3 and the governance principle on attributable rota changes, but US-1 AC2
  and the Overview's "reaches within seconds... settle the same day" framing need rewriting
  since acceptance alone no longer settles anything.
- **C. Two-stage: accepted swap is provisional and shown as pending on both staff's views;
  it lands on the published rota only at manager approval** — reconciles US-1/US-2 (staff see
  the exchange as agreed) with US-3 (manager still gates the rota), at the cost of a new
  "provisional" state the entities model doesn't currently have.

**Q2 — Eligibility on the counterpart's shift (blocks build, F2).** Should a swap check that
each person is eligible for the shift they're taking on?
- **A. Block the swap request at creation** if the counterpart isn't eligible for the
  requester's shift (or vice versa) — cheapest to reason about, matches "the rota builder
  already refuses" pattern from decision 0003.
- **B. Allow the request, but block it at manager approval** with the reason shown — keeps
  staff-to-staff negotiation free, defers the gate to the point a human is already reviewing
  (US-3), consistent with FR-006's approval step if Q1 resolves to B or C.
- **C. No system check — managers are trusted to catch it at approval without tooling
  support** — cheapest to build, but leaves the exact gap decision 0003 was written to close.

**Q3 — Open-shift overlap (F3).** FEAT-006's draft "offer to all staff, first-claim" bullet
duplicates FEAT-005 Open Shifts and contradicts shift-swaps' own out-of-scope line.
- **A. Strip that bullet from FEAT-006 entirely** — shift-swaps stays one-for-one exchange
  only, matching spec.md's Intent; open/first-claim behavior stays FEAT-005's alone.
- **B. Fold shift-swaps into FEAT-005 as a related capability** (e.g. `composes-with` or
  `depends-on`) instead of restating FEAT-005's description inside FEAT-006.
- **C. Keep both as currently drafted** — not recommended; flagged here only to make the
  cost of doing nothing visible.

**Q4 — Reaching staff who can't be reached by push (F4).** How should a swap request reach a
colleague who has no push notifications enabled (roughly two-thirds of staff, per the 2026
device survey)?
- **A. Apply decision 0007 as-is**: SMS fallback when push isn't available, and flag the
  manager when neither channel exists — no new channel, just wire the existing rule into
  the swap-request and swap-answer notifications explicitly.
- **B. Push-only, accept slower answers for staff without it** — cheaper, but the Overview's
  "seconds" framing and SC-001's 24-hour/80% target should then be reworded against the
  survey's real reach, not against a "everyone has push" premise.
- **C. Add a venue-tablet or noticeboard surface for swap requests** — addresses the 31% who
  rely on the noticeboard today, but is new scope beyond what any story currently asks for.

## Recommended disposition

Not a pass/fail clearance — the lead rules on it — but as gap-finding input: **do not move
this spec into design/prototype work yet.** F1 and F2 are build-blocking contradictions/gaps
on their own; F3 additionally means the feature-map derivation cannot be taken as-is without
creating a duplicate, self-contradicting capability. F4–F7 will each cause rework if picked
up after build starts rather than now.

## Strengths

Traceability is genuinely good — every FR cites its source story or decision (bar the two
flagged in F5), the Feature Selection and Filter Rejections tables are explicit and legible,
and the spec surfaces its own open questions (device caching, request-count limits) rather
than hiding them.
