## Clarifications needed

### C1: Charging a funded (free-school-meal) order (G1)

**Question**: How should a funded child's order be charged, given the family may have no card on file and no wallet balance?
**Options**: 1. Funded orders skip the wallet entirely — zero charge, order still recorded for the kitchen and allergy flag, funding claimed by the school outside Tally · 2. Funded orders still post against a wallet that can sit at exactly £0.00, backed by a school-level fund the office tops up · 3. Funded status is per-meal, so a family with both funded and paid children is charged only for the paid ones
**Why it matters**: Without this, roughly 59% of pilot-school pupils (universal infant FSM plus means-tested KS2) cannot place an order under FR-003 as written, and the governance floor's "or when the meal is funded... no charge applies" clause has no implementation.

### C2: Are hidden menu items ever shown to parents? (G2)

**Question**: Should a parent ever see a menu item the kitchen has marked hidden?
**Options**: 1. No — drop FR-002's "including hidden items" clause; parents see only the non-hidden published menu, matching menu-admin's in-flight guarantee · 2. Yes — redefine "hidden" as hidden from the default view only, and amend FEAT-002's acceptance criterion to match · 3. Split by reason (e.g. sold-out items shown greyed, discontinued items fully hidden)
**Why it matters**: menu-admin is being built right now specifically to guarantee hidden items never reach parents; shipping FR-002 as written breaks that guarantee the moment lunch-orders lands.

### C3: Does the wallet capability really go back to `proposed`? (G3)

**Question**: Should FEAT-003 (Wallet) regress to `proposed` for this rework, or stay `delivered` with a scoped delta?
**Options**: 1. Keep `delivered`, add an in-flight work row scoped to "charge at order confirmation" (mirrors FEAT-002's `live` row) · 2. Regress to `proposed` map-wide, as derivation.md currently proposes · 3. Split: keep top-up/balance `delivered`, mint a dependent FEAT for order-time charging
**Why it matters**: top-up and balance display aren't being rebuilt; rolling the whole capability back to `proposed` overstates what's unbuilt and could stall unrelated work that reasonably assumes "wallet is delivered."

### C4: Squaring up the Screens & Flows manifest before build (G4)

**Question**: The prototype has dead links (order history, payment confirmation) and an orphan screen (allergen key) with a mis-cited flow. How should this be resolved before build?
**Options**: 1. Build the missing pieces now — a greyed-but-reachable order-history screen (per FEAT-006's deferred status), a real confirmation screen with an SCR-ID rendering the held/top-up state, a manifest row for the allergen key (or fold it into SCR-001) — and repoint FLOW-003 at US-4 · 2. Strip the dangling nav links and rows out of the prototype and spec until FEAT-006 and the confirmation step are actually in scope · 3. Accept the gaps as known debt and proceed
**Why it matters**: no P1 scenario can be confirmed walkable end-to-end while the confirmation step and order history dead-end, and the prototype's own wallet-short demo data silently disappears instead of demonstrating the one flow (insufficient funds) the story set calls out by name.

### C5: Is meal rating in scope? (G5)

**Question**: Should meal rating stay in this spec?
**Options**: 1. Cut FR-009 and the matching FEAT-005 extent line — no story supports it · 2. Write the missing user story and screen for it now · 3. Move it to Open Questions as a future-spec candidate instead of a MUST requirement
**Why it matters**: an untraceable MUST requirement with no story or screen either gets built off-spec or silently dropped, and currently overstates FEAT-005's extent on the map.

### C6: Tightening the unmeasurable criteria (G6)

**Question**: How should SC-002, FR-010, and SC-003 be made measurable and correctly homed, and should FR-007's regulatory claim be verified before build?
**Options**: 1. Fold FR-010 into a measurable SC using the Technical-notes p95 figure, replace SC-002 with a concrete proxy metric, re-home SC-003 to FEAT-004 (deferred), and add a `verified:`/`memory-asserted` line to FR-007 · 2. Keep SC-002/FR-010 qualitative for now and tighten at build time · 3. Drop SC-003 from this spec's success criteria entirely until FEAT-004 ships
**Why it matters**: an unmeasurable SC can't gate release, SC-003 asks this spec to prove something an unbuilt sibling feature owns, and an unverified regulatory claim ("School Food Standards require...") is a floor-class external claim this round could not confirm or refute.

### C7: What happens to a held order? (G7)

**Question**: What is the rule for an order held on insufficient wallet balance (US-1 scenario 3)?
**Options**: 1. A held order auto-cancels if not topped up by the weekly cut-off, freeing the kitchen count · 2. A held order counts toward the kitchen's count optimistically until the cut-off, then cancels (with a parent notification) if still unfunded · 3. A held order is never counted or sent to the kitchen sheet until actually paid, even if that means it silently disappears if the parent forgets
**Why it matters**: the kitchen buys stock from the cut-off counts (`0003-order-cutoff.md`); an uncounted or double-counted held order either wastes food or leaves a child without a meal.
