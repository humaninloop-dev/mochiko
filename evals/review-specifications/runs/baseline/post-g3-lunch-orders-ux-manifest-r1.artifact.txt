## Clarifications

1. **Hidden items on the parent-facing week menu.** menu-admin (in-flight, accepted) says a
   hidden item must never reach a parent-facing response. lunch-orders FR-002 says the opposite.
   - **(a)** lunch-orders defers to menu-admin: hidden items never appear on SCR-001 at all.
     Simplest, no new UI state, matches an already-shipped safety/stock decision.
   - **(b)** Hidden items appear but visibly unavailable (no price/allergen action, can't be
     selected) — "nothing on offer is missed" is satisfied a different way. Needs a new display
     rule and a menu-admin amendment, since menu-admin's FR-002 currently forbids *any* form of
     display.
   - **(c)** lunch-orders overrides menu-admin for this one screen. Reopens a shipped safety
     decision (a recalled item becoming visible again) — needs the same sign-off menu-admin got.
   - *Impact:* (a) ships as-is; (b)/(c) both require touching a decision that's already in
     production behavior.

2. **Which cut-off governs a mid-week change?** Thursday 17:00 the week before (FR-005,
   decision 0003) or Sunday midnight (US-1 scenario 2)?
   - **(a)** Thursday 17:00 is the only cut-off; fix the story's wording. No new logic.
   - **(b)** Sunday midnight is a distinct, earlier soft deadline for changes only (Thursday
     stays the hard cut-off for new orders) — needs its own FR, its own refusal message, and a
     reason two clocks exist.
   - **(c)** Decision 0003 itself needs revisiting for changes vs. new orders. Reopens a ratified
     decision with the catering contractor and touches the Friday wholesaler-order rationale.
   - *Impact:* (a) is a one-line story fix; (b)/(c) add a second time boundary parents and the
     kitchen both have to track.

3. **Is multi-child single-checkout, or meal rating, actually in scope for this run?**
   - **(a)** Drop both — trim FEAT-005's extent and FR-009 to match the stories that exist
     (US-1, US-2, US-4, all single-child, no rating).
   - **(b)** Keep multi-child checkout as real scope: write its story, FR, and a screen/flow
     showing one combined charge across children; drop rating (no source story at all).
   - **(c)** Keep both — write the missing story, FR, SC, and screens for each before this spec
     leaves draft.
   - *Impact:* (a) ships the smallest, fully-specified slice; (b)/(c) each add build-now scope
     that presently has zero design behind it.

4. **The prototype's dead ends (order history, payment confirmation, cancel/change,
   insufficient-wallet hold) — finish before build, or explicitly waive?**
   - **(a)** Author the missing pieces now: an `scr-004-order-history.html` page, a numbered
     confirmation screen, and flows for cancel/change and the top-up hold, before the spec exits
     draft.
   - **(b)** Waive order-history and the wallet-hold flow to a follow-up spec, and narrow
     FLOW-001/FLOW-002's claims to what's actually built (drop the "→ confirmation" and
     "→ SCR-004" legs).
   - **(c)** Leave as-is and let engineering invent the cancel/refund and low-wallet UX at build
     time.
   - *Impact:* (a)/(b) keep the "walkable, no dead ends" bar the spec claims to meet; (c) hands
     safety- and money-adjacent UX decisions (wallet floor, cut-off floor) to whoever writes the
     code, unreviewed.

## Minor (logged, not blocking)

- FR-007's "as the School Food Standards require" is an undisclosed external legal claim — no
  citation given for which School Food Standards clause requires allergen display before
  ordering; worth a source line even though the underlying requirement is plausible.
- SC-002 "Parents find ordering easy" has no metric or measurement method, unlike SC-001,
  SC-003, and SC-004 which all name one.
- Every row in the Screens & Flows table (`spec.md:97-106`) carries `—` in the Feature column
  even though derivation has already run and FEAT-005/FEAT-006 exist — these should be
  FEAT-tagged now that selection is decided.
- Open question "how many weeks ahead may a parent order?" may already be answered by "the
  kitchen publishes a week's menu" (FEAT-002) — if only one week is ever published ahead, this
  question dissolves; worth confirming before leaving it open.

## Recommended verdict

**needs-revision** — offered as input to the run's lead, not a self-issued clearing. F1, F2,
and F6 each reopen or contradict a decision already ratified or shipped elsewhere in the
product; F3, F4, and F5 mean the UX-bearing half of this spec cannot currently be walked
end-to-end for its two highest-priority stories. None of the seven look like they need new
discovery — they read as fixable by reconciling this spec against the map, the story files, and
the docs/ decisions the product already has.

## Strengths

The allergy-safety mechanic is the one thread that holds together end-to-end once F4's citation
is fixed: US-4's acceptance criteria, FR-007's per-item allergen requirement, and SCR-001's
struck-through/disabled radio inputs for Ada's milk allergy all agree with each other and with
`docs/free-school-meals.md`'s note that a funded or allergic child still needs a real order —
this is the strongest-modelled corner of the spec.
