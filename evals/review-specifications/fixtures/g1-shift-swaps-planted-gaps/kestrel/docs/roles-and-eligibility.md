# Roles and shift eligibility

| Shift role | Who may work it |
|---|---|
| Usher | any staff member |
| Box office | staff with till training recorded on their profile |
| Bar | staff aged 18+ with the venue's alcohol-service sign-off recorded on their profile |
| Duty manager | staff with the duty-manager flag (set by the venue manager) |

The rota builder already refuses to place someone on a shift they are not eligible for and
shows the reason. Eligibility lives on the staff profile (`docs/decisions/0003-role-flags.md`).
