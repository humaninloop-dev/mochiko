# Staff devices and app use — 2026 survey

Survey of 612 front-of-house staff across 29 venues, March 2026.

| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Has the Kestrel app installed | 69 % |
| Uses the app while on shift | 41 % (phones are locked away at 17 venues during shows) |
| Has push notifications enabled | 52 % of those with the app installed |
| Relies on SMS or the noticeboard for rota changes | 31 % |
| Shares a phone with a family member | 4 % |

Practical reading: a message that only lands as a push notification reaches about a third of
staff while they are at work. Anything time-critical goes by SMS as well, per decision 0007.
