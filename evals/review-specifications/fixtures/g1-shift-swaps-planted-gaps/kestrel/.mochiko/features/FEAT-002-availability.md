# FEAT-002 — Availability

> Status: delivered
> since 2026-04-06 · sticky — live rows may still be visible below

## Capability

Staff declare the days and hours they can work, week by week or as a standing pattern; the
rota draft flags any shift that falls outside a person's availability.

## Extent

- Standing weekly pattern plus per-week overrides; a manager sees availability per venue.
- Not: the rota refusing to place someone outside their availability — it flags, the manager
  decides.
