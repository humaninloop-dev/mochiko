# Payment methods — August 2026

| Method | Share of active subscriptions | How and when the money is taken |
|---|---|---|
| Card (Stripe) | 72 % | charged at the weekly cut-off, Friday 12:00, for Monday's box |
| Direct Debit (GoCardless) | 22 % | collection lodged the Thursday before the cut-off, settles the following Tuesday; a lodged collection cannot be pulled back |
| PayPal | 6 % | charged at the weekly cut-off like a card |

Direct Debit customers see the charge on their statement up to five days after the box has
been delivered. A collection that has been lodged and then needs reversing is a refund, not a
cancellation, and takes three to five working days to land.
