# FEAT-001 — Box subscription

> Status: delivered
> since 2025-11-03 · sticky — live rows may still be visible below

## Capability

A customer subscribes to a box size, is charged weekly by their chosen payment method, and can
cancel; the account page shows every charge with its box and date.

## Extent

- Small, medium, large boxes; weekly charge at the Friday cut-off; card, Direct Debit, PayPal.
- Cancel from the account page, effective after the next paid box.
- Not: stopping boxes temporarily without cancelling.
