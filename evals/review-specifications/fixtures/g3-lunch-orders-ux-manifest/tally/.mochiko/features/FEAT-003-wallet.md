# FEAT-003 — Wallet

> Status: delivered
> since 2026-05-18 · sticky — live rows may still be visible below

## Capability

A parent tops up a family wallet by card; meals are paid from the wallet; a funded meal costs
the family nothing and the school claims it.

## Extent

- Top-up by card in whole pounds; balance shown on every parent screen; a low-balance email.
- A funded child's meals are recorded at zero for the family and claimed by the school.
- Not: refunds to card — a credit stays in the wallet.
