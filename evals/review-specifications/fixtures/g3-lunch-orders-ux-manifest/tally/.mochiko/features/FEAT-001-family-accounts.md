# FEAT-001 — Family accounts

> Status: delivered
> since 2026-02-09 · sticky — live rows may still be visible below

## Capability

A parent signs in and sees the children linked to their account, each with school, class,
recorded dietary needs and allergies, and free-school-meal status.

## Extent

- Children linked by the school office from the school's roll; two parents may share a family.
- Dietary needs and allergies entered by the parent and confirmed by the school office.
- Not: anything ordered or paid — the account is who, not what.
