# 0003 — Weekly order cut-off

Decided 2026-04-15 · founder + schools lead + the catering contractor · status: in force

## Decision

- Orders and changes for a school week close at **17:00 on the Thursday of the week before**.
- After the cut-off, a parent cannot place or change an order for that week; the school office
  can, by phone to the kitchen, for a new starter or a leaver only.

## Why

The kitchen places its stock order with the wholesaler on Friday morning from the counts. A
later cut-off means cooking to a guess.
