# Free school meals at Tally schools

- Every pupil in Reception, Year 1, and Year 2 gets a free lunch (universal infant free school
  meals). At the pilot schools that is 41 % of pupils.
- A further 18 % of Key Stage 2 pupils are eligible for means-tested free school meals.
- For a funded meal nothing is charged to the family; the school claims the funding. Many of
  these families have no card on file and no wallet balance, and some have never topped up.
- A funded child still needs an order placed so the kitchen has a count and the allergy flag.
