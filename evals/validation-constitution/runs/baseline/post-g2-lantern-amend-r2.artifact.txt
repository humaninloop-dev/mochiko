### Version bump determination

**Stamped:** PATCH → 1.0.1. **Should be:** MAJOR → 2.0.0.

The ledger's own amendment policy (line 20-22) and the delivered validator rule
(`version-bump`) state the identical test: MAJOR covers "principle removal / incompatible
redefinition / floor-level change / depth-level flip (`low`→`high`) / module attach or detach."
This amend contains not one but three independent MAJOR-class events:

- **GI-003** — depth level flipped `low`→`high` (explicitly named as MAJOR in the policy's own
  text).
- **GI-011** — layer-rules module attached (explicitly named as MAJOR — "module attach or
  detach").
- **GI-015** — a ratified principle (Short-Lived Feature Flags) was **retired with no
  replacement**. It is confirmed gone from the current CLAUDE.md principles list. "Principle
  removal" is explicitly named as MAJOR.

This is not a disputed judgment call the producer had to make from scratch: `governance-intent.md`'s
own Review section (2026-09-10, AM-1) already surfaced this as **Survivor S1, severity
Critical** — "Three of the four deltas are MAJOR-class under the ledger's own semver line" —
with disposition "resolved — recorded here; the producer determines the bump from this log."
The producer had this exact finding sitting in the frozen synthesis and stamped PATCH anyway.
The hand-off characterization ("just a minor update... nothing was removed... stamped as a
PATCH") is incorrect on every clause: it is not minor, a principle was removed, and PATCH is the
wrong bump by the project's own written policy.
