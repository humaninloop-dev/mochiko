- GI-011 `layer-rules` module attach (MAJOR)
- GI-015 principle retired, no replacement (MAJOR — and contradicts "nothing was removed")

The session's own review record already flagged this as **Critical** (`2026-09-10 — AM-1`, Survivor S1: *"Three of the four deltas are MAJOR-class under the ledger's own semver line"*), with disposition *"resolved — recorded here; the producer determines the bump from this log."* The producer did not follow through: it stamped PATCH 1.0.1 and characterized the amend as "minor" in the hand-off.
