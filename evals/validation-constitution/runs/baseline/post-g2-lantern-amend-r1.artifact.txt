   unrelated "GI-013 — Signed Commits" principle marked `Trace: GI-013 (minted)`, and CLAUDE.md

   be GPG-signed" and line 49 "Evolution notes... "). This is an ID collision hiding an
   ungoverned principle addition — it has no synthesis backing, no mention in the amendment
   log's GI-delta column, and reuses an ID already spoken for. FAIL-eligible on its own.
2. **Generic Thresholds — GI-005.** The AM-1 high-row ruling in the synthesis is concrete:
   `--cov-fail-under=60` blocking, ≥80% warning. Neither CLAUDE.md's quality-gate line nor the
   ledger's GI-005 enforcement/testability text carries any number — both still say "an

3. **Missing Enforcement — GI-011 layer-rules.** The importlinter layers contract and the
   domain-dependency registry (seeded `pydantic`/`attrs`) are asserted as landed, but their
   named home, `.claude/rules/mochiko/layers.md`, does not exist. There is nowhere for the
   registry block to live.

No excess-governance instances found beyond the above (i.e., no principle restating a constraint
already homed elsewhere with no other defect).
