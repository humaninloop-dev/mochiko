### Issues requiring fix (ranked by severity)

1. **[CRITICAL] Waiver of a legal-mandate obligation.** `governance-ledger.md` Waivers table: GI-012 waives the PCI-DSS quarterly ASV scan (Requirement 11.3.2). The checklist is unconditional: a waiver naming a legal-mandate module obligation is a FAIL, no exception. The synthesis's own review caught this and marked it Critical; the founder overruling the reviewer doesn't cure it — a legal-mandate obligation isn't the founder's or the session's to waive by ruling, that's the entire point of the unwaivable stratum. **Fix:** remove the GI-012 row from the ledger's Waivers table. If the ASV cadence genuinely can't be met pre-launch, that's a compliance decision to take outside this mechanism (e.g., actually schedule the first scan before the card-data-in-scope launch, or get real legal sign-off that SAQ D permits the gap) — not something a governance waiver row can paper over.

2. **[CRITICAL] Cardholder-data rules file doesn't cover where the PAN first lands.** `.claude/rules/mochiko/cardholder-data.md` scopes only `src/payments/**/*.ts`. Both `README.md` and the ratified GI-009 text agree the PAN transits Tessellate's own `src/checkout/` handler before it ever reaches `src/payments/`'s tokeniser — the synthesis's own review (S2) already caught this exact scope gap and "resolved" it in the element text, but the rules-file glob was never updated to match. As shipped, editing or creating a file under `src/checkout/` never injects the "PAN must not be logged/persisted/queued, functions must be named `*Untokenised`" rules — exactly where a raw card number is handled first. **Fix:** add `"src/checkout/**/*.ts"` to the file's `paths` list.

3. **[HIGH] Literal placeholder trace stamp.** CLAUDE.md, the WCAG/a11y principle line: `<!-- GI-XXX -->` instead of `<!-- GI-010 -->`. This is the No-Placeholders-Rule anti-pattern by name. **Fix:** correct to `GI-010`.

4. **[HIGH] Ratified stamp omits an attached legal-mandate module.** CLAUDE.md's `**Ratified:**` line: `modules: gdpr · a11y` — `pci-dss` is missing, despite being attached (ledger header, fact profile, and GI-009's own principle all agree it's attached). A reader who only reads the stamp line would conclude Tessellate carries no PCI-DSS governance. **Fix:** `modules: gdpr · pci-dss · a11y`.

5. **[HIGH] GI-016 inverts a ratified exclusion.** The synthesis's Deliberate Exclusions section records GI-016 as *"Documentation language and style are not governed... nobody is going to police it"* (Inês, dimension 10, Confident). The delivered set instead mints a principle under the same ID — "All internal documentation MUST be written in British English," fully enforced via `cspell`/en-GB in CI. This isn't a drift in wording; it's the opposite ruling, filed under the ID of the thing it contradicts. **Fix:** delete the GI-016 principle line, its Quality-gates implications, and its ledger Three-Part record. The exclusion in the synthesis needs no principle tracing to it — that's what "excluded" means.

6. **[MEDIUM] Coverage blocking threshold doesn't match the declared row, and no override is recorded.** The floor card's `high` row: "≥80% warning, ≥60% blocking (session-overridable)." The synthesis's own Floor Expression table records the high row explicitly with **"no session override."** The delivered principle (CLAUDE.md, Quality gates, and ledger GI-005) enforces ≥70% blocking — a number matching neither the card row nor a recorded override. **Fix:** either change the blocking threshold to 60% to match the ratified, unoverridden row, or take an explicit session override to Inês and record it in `governance-intent.md` before keeping 70%.

7. **[MEDIUM] GI-011 drops the ratified, numeric commitment.** The synthesis records a Confident, fully specified minted principle: LCP ≤2.5s at p75 on the product and checkout pages, measured by Lighthouse CI on every PR, blocking merge on regression — the founder's own number from prior bounce data. The delivered region line reads "Pages SHOULD load quickly" (no threshold, no tool, `SHOULD` not `MUST`). The ledger's Enforcement/Testability sections for GI-011 are equally non-measurable ("Engineers watch the Lighthouse scores" / "Pass: pages feel quick. Fail: complaints."), and `npm run lhci` — present in the synthesis's Real Commands table — never appears in CLAUDE.md's Quality gates. **Fix:** restate GI-011 with the 2.5s/p75/Lighthouse-CI/merge-blocking language from the synthesis; add `npm run lhci` to Quality gates; rewrite the ledger's Enforcement/Testability to match.

8. **[MEDIUM] Ledger is missing the required Exception registry section.** Shape 3 requires: Governance Floor header · Waivers · Amendment policy · **Exception registry** · Three-Part records · amendment log. No `## Exception registry` heading exists in `governance-ledger.md` (Waivers is a distinct, separate section in the shape, not a substitute). **Fix:** add the section — an empty table with "[none yet]" is sufficient if there's nothing to record.

9. **[LOW] GI-004 is claimed by two principle-index lines.** CLAUDE.md carries both "Secrets MUST stay out of the repository..." and "Security at boundaries — see `.claude/rules/mochiko/security.md`" tagged `<!-- GI-004 -->`, and the ledger's single GI-004 record conflates both bodies of content (secrets management and boundary auth) under one Three-Part entry. This breaks "one line per principle." **Fix:** either give boundary-auth its own GI-ID (add a row to the trace summary and ledger) or collapse the "Secrets" line's substantive content into `security.md` and leave a single companion-pointer line in the region, matching the pattern already used for GI-009/GI-013/GI-014.
