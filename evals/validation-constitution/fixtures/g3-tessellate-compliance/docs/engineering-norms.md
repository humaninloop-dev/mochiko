# Engineering norms (living doc, started 2025-11)

Not rules, just how we like to work. Edit freely.

- Small PRs. If it needs a paragraph to explain, split it.
- Prefer boring technology. We have enough interesting problems.
- Write the test you wish you had when the bug comes back.
- Name things for what they are, not what they do for you.
- When in doubt about payments, ask in `#payments` before you write code.
- Fridays after 3 pm are for docs, tidying, and not deploying.
