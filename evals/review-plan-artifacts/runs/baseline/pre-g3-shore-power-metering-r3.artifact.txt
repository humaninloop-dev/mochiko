**Question**: The signed architecture (store-delta.md, AX-013) and this round's constraints-and-decisions.md (C-005, D-004) assert opposite vendor capabilities, and both are explicitly unverified against real Enerlink documentation ("memory-asserted"). Which is true?
**Options**: 1. Push is real — keep the signed architecture and SPN-007/AX-013 as ruled; retract C-005/D-004 and rewrite the ingest decision to match. 2. Pull-only is real — re-open AX-013, redesign meter-ingest as a poller, rewrite contracts/api.yaml and tasks.md Cycle 1 off the push shape, and take FR-002/SC-001's 5-minute target back to product since hourly pull cannot meet it. 3. Neither has been checked — verify against Enerlink's actual v3 docs before either artifact is trusted further.
**Why it matters**: this single fact decides the container topology (SPN-007's existence and shape), the gateway contract's auth/method, the walking-skeleton test, and whether FR-002/SC-001 are achievable at all — every other finding in this round downstream of ingest mode inherits whichever answer is wrong.
