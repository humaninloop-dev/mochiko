## Notes for the lead

Two of the above (F1, F2) are worth reading past the table.

**F1** is the one I'd escalate hardest: the single test that exists for US-2/FR-003 — the P1
pricing story, on a feature the spec itself marks "high (billing)" rigor — asserts the exact wrong
number the story's own acceptance scenario calls out by name as the failure mode. If Cycle 4 is
implemented to make its own assert pass, it ships the bug.

**F2** is a structural one, not a wording one. Round 1's G3 asked the technical-analyst to justify
D-004's pull choice against C-005; the round-2 rewrite did that, cleanly — but in doing so it
revealed that D-004 was never reconciled with the signed store-delta.md, which rules push, gives
push its own container (SPN-007), and grounds that ruling in the same FR-002 5-minute target D-004
now says push can't meet. That store delta is signed — it isn't mine to re-open, and I'd flag to the
lead that reopening it is very likely the actual next step here, not a contracts/tasks patch. I
did not take a position on which of push/pull is correct (that's an Enerlink-capability question,
not a design-review one); I'm reporting that the package currently asserts both, unreconciled,
across four artifacts.

I was asked not to re-review data-model.md, store-delta.md, or quickstart.md in depth, and did not —
F3 and F9 surfaced from the spot-check the incremental mode calls for (presence and decisions_honored,
respectively), not a full pass.
