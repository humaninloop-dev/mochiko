## Clarifications needed

### C1: Does marketing consent gate the offer e-mail? (G5)

**Question**: C-006 leaves open whether the offer-issued notice is a transactional e-mail (no consent gate) or a marketing-adjacent one (consent required) under the counterparty-confidentiality principle and UK GDPR.
**Options**: 1. Treat it as strictly transactional (booking-adjacent, no consent gate) · 2. Require marketing consent before sending · 3. Split the notice into a transactional core plus an optional marketing addendum
**Why it matters**: it decides whether the Postmark template and the send-path gating logic exist at all before Cycle 2 builds `mail.offer-issued`; it is also the [NEEDS CLARIFICATION] marker the tier-1 pre-assert flags as unresolved.

## Failure narrative

**What failed**: the design-phase output package for FEAT-031 does not clear — verdict `critical-gaps`.

**Why**: two independent failure modes stack. First, material divergence: the package carries an entity (Notification), an endpoint (`GET /berths/{berthId}/availability-forecast`), and a cycle card (Cycle 4) tracing to FR-007/US-5/FR-009, none of which exist in `spec.md` or in the sufficiency report's gap list (G-01–G-04) — this alone is an auto-fail under the material-divergence floor, independent of any count. Second, on the scope the gap list did name, two of the four gaps (G-01's marina-queue-view surface, G-04's NFR target) were left open, a cycle-card oracle in Cycle 3 asserts the exact re-offer timing FR-005 and US-3 explicitly forbid, a NEEDS CLARIFICATION marker survives on a blocking constraint, a declared-sensitive field ships unclassified, and a technology decision (D-005, Redis TTL) contradicts the standing blocking single-datastore ruling with no sign-off recorded.

**What was tried**: ran the tier-1 automated pre-assert (`check-artifacts.py`) over `constraints-and-decisions.md`, `data-model.md`, and `tasks.md`, which independently confirmed the unresolved marker (G5) and the unclassified `contactEmail` field (G6) as ground truth; walked the Analysis, Data Model, API Contracts, and Cross-Artifact Consistency checklists from `references/ARTIFACT-CHECKLISTS.md` against the supplied package; cross-read `spec.md`, its four story files, the sufficiency report, and the product baselines (`data-model.md`, `constraints-and-decisions.md`, `architecture/spine.md`, `architecture/concerns.md`) to establish traceability and constraint conformance. No store-delta checklist applied — the sufficiency report's no-delta claim stands and no delta was authored in this package.

## Cross-artifact concern routed to review-feasibility

Whether Redis key-TTL expiry (D-005) can actually deliver the "exact to the second" precision the decision claims, and whether the `offer_locks` polling design (D-004, 500 ms worst-case latency) can meet the SC-002 60s p95 target once G-03's serialisation load is real, are buildability questions for `mochiko:review-feasibility` — this review's finding (G7) is limited to the decision's contradiction with the standing AX-002/C-003 constraint, not whether the mechanism works.

## Spec gaps observed (advisory — requested by the requirements analyst; not part of this verdict)

- **FR-006 / US-4 mismatch**: FR-006 lists "position, date range, vessel length" as what marina staff see; US-4's own Given/When/Then adds "whether an offer is open." The FR is narrower than its own story — worth reconciling so the eventual endpoint's response shape isn't under-specified.
- **No latency success criterion for re-offer after decline/lapse**: SC-002 measures only the *first* offer after a berth frees. FR-005/US-3 require the *next* offer after a decline or lapse "immediately," but no SC gives that path a measurable target the way SC-002 does for the initial offer — plausibly part of why Cycle 3's TEST oracle (G4) went wrong.
- **Hold-expiry-during-retry interaction underspecified**: the edge case "saved payment method is declined on accept: the offer stays open for the remainder of the hold" doesn't say what happens if the 24h hold lapses mid-retry — does the retry window ever close before the hold's natural expiry, or can an owner retry indefinitely until acceptance succeeds?
- **Multiple concurrent waitlist entries per owner is implied, not stated**: the design phase's validation rule (one `waiting`/`offered` entry per (ownerId, marinaId, berthClass, fromDate, toDate)) implies an owner may hold entries across different berth classes or marinas simultaneously, but the spec never states this policy — worth an explicit line so a future design doesn't need to infer it from a validation rule.
