## Notes for the lead

**On scope.** Per the incremental protocol, `tasks.md`, `contracts/api.yaml`,
`constraints-and-decisions.md`, and `design-disclosure.md` got the full walk; `data-model.md` and
`store-delta.md` (the tech lead's round-1 territory, the latter user-signed) got the
consistency-only spot-check the protocol allows — entity names, schemas, decisions-honored,
architecture conformance. That spot-check is what surfaced G5 and G8: the signed store delta
disagrees with the re-issued constraints doc on the one fact the whole ingest design turns on.
Per `review-plan-artifacts.escalate-full-reread`, two or more consistency issues in one prior
artifact escalate it to a full re-read — `store-delta.md` has taken two hits here (G5, G8) from
two different new-set artifacts, so I'd treat it as needing that full re-read next round even
though it's signed, rather than continuing to spot-check it. `quickstart.md` couldn't be
spot-checked at all — it isn't there (G9).

**On the verdict.** G7 (the consumption-export endpoint) is textbook material divergence — new
capability tracing to a story that doesn't exist, closing no named gap, covered by no cycle card
— which auto-fails the package under this skill's own floor regardless of how the rest scores.
G5 and G6 would each independently push toward `critical-gaps` on their own: G5 because the
package can't be built as written (three artifacts assume push, one says push is impossible),
G6 because the one billing acceptance test in the whole package validates the exact wrong
arithmetic the story it cites was written to rule out. None of these are close calls or matters
of taste.

**On what's genuinely better.** The round-1 fixes were real, not cosmetic — G1-G4 all landed
correctly on their own terms (see `strengths`). The problems above are new or newly surfaced by
this round's changes, not a rehash of round 1.

**Honesty caveat, repeated from the frontmatter:** the canonical checklist/severity/verdict files
this skill points to, and the Tier-1 pre-assert script, were not reachable from this sandbox.
Every finding above is a direct citation against the named artifacts; only the severity labels
and the exact verdict thresholds are this reviewer's best-effort substitute for the canonical
grammar. Recommend re-confirming the verdict mapping once those files are reachable.
