## Process concerns

The cover note asked for three things this pass does not do, and why:

1. **No `fixed/` patches or in-place edits.** A reviewer that authors the fix is grading its own authorship, not reviewing — this holds even for the "five-minute" CreditNote add, which would in fact entrench G1 rather than resolve it. All fixes above are routed back to the producing seats.
2. **`data-model.md` got the full checklist, not a skim.** design-disclosure.md does list this seat as its (covering) author, which is itself G12 — but treating that as grounds to look less closely is the exact shortcut the review is supposed to prevent, and it's what would have hidden G5 (unclassified PII) and G8 (missing Validation Rules) — both confirmed by the Tier-1 automated checker, not just judgment calls.
3. **Verdict is not `ready`.** The store delta is not informational: it diverges materially from the sufficiency report's no-delta claim (G3), and it introduces a second datastore without its required sign-off recorded in the artifact that would carry it (G2) — a production-before-go-live concern, not a backlog item. Eight Critical findings put this at `critical-gaps` mechanically, before any judgment call about severity.
