## Clarifications needed

### C1: Is the offer e-mail transactional or marketing-gated? (G8)

**Question**: C-006 leaves this open with a live [NEEDS CLARIFICATION] marker. Does the offer notice require marketing consent, or is it sent regardless as a transactional message tied to a time-bound hold?
**Options**: 1. Transactional — sent to every entrant who receives an offer, no consent gate. 2. Marketing-gated — suppressed for owners who have opted out of marketing e-mail. 3. Split — a minimal transactional stub (hold deadline only) always sent, richer content gated by consent.
**Why it matters**: This determines whether FR-003/FR-004's offer flow can silently fail to notify an entrant (if gated and consent is absent), which would break the 24-hour hold's practical usability and SC-002/SC-003.

## Appendix — spec-layer observations (for the requirements analyst; out of scope for this review's verdict)

Per the requirements-analyst's ask, flagging what surfaced against `spec.md` while reviewing the design package. These are not part of the critical-gaps verdict above and are not this skill's jurisdiction (`mochiko:review-specifications` owns spec review) — they're offered as raw input, not a graded finding:

- **No FR covers owner notification.** The Notification entity exists because *someone* judged owners need to be told about offer-issued and entry-expired events (the edge case text says "the owner is told"), but no FR says so. FR-007 was invented downstream (G10) precisely because there was no real requirement to cite. Worth authoring an FR for "the system MUST notify the owner when X" (join confirmation? offer issued? entry expired?) so the design layer has something real to trace to.
- **Marketing-consent question (C-006) traces back to a spec silence.** The spec never states whether offer notices are purely transactional; that ambiguity is what let C-006 ship with a live clarification marker. Worth a line in spec.md's constraints or FR-003 saying the offer notice is a transactional message independent of marketing consent (if that's the intent).
- **No FR addresses concurrent/overlapping waitlist entries for the same owner across different-but-overlapping date ranges** — only the exact-match dedup rule shows up, and only in data-model.md's Validation Rules (design layer), not the spec. If overlapping-but-not-identical ranges are meant to be allowed or blocked, that's a product decision the spec should make explicit rather than leaving it to whatever the data model happens to encode.
- **FR-004's "saved payment method" assumes one exists.** The edge cases cover a *declined* card but not an owner with *no* saved method at all when an offer lands — worth an explicit line on what happens then (offer stays open and owner is prompted to add one? treated as a decline?).
