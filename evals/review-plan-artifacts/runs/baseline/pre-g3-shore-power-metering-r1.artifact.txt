## Clarifications needed

### C1: Does Enerlink v3 push or does Halyard have to pull? (G1, G2)

**Question**: The signed architecture (store-delta.md) and this round's re-issued contract and Cycle 1 all build a push-receiving endpoint. This round's re-issued constraints-and-decisions.md (C-005, D-004) asserts the opposite — that Enerlink v3 has no push at all, pull-only, hourly. Which is actually true of the vendor API?
**Options**: 1. Push exists as signed — C-005/D-004 are wrong and should be dropped, restoring the original push-based ingest and its 5-minute freshness story. 2. Pull-only is correct — the signed architecture (SPN-007–009, AX-013), the contract, and Cycle 1 all need reopening and rebuilding around an hourly-pull worker, and FR-002/SC-001's 5-minute target needs to be renegotiated against the resulting ~60-minute freshness ceiling. 3. Something in between (e.g. push exists but is unreliable, hence a pull fallback) that neither artifact currently describes.
**Why it matters**: This isn't a wording gap — it determines which container design, which contract shape, and which walking-skeleton test are correct. Building against the wrong one wastes the whole first cycle and leaves AX-013's signed ruling stale.

### C2: Is the finance CSV export actually in scope for this feature round? (G6)

**Question**: `/marinas/{marinaId}/consumption-export` cites a user story (US-6) that doesn't exist in this spec. Was this requested through a channel outside the specify phase (e.g. a direct ask to the design seat, per design-disclosure.md's rung-4 note), and if so should it be traced to a real story before this round closes, or held back to a later one?
**Options**: 1. Add a US-5/US-6 to the spec retroactively and re-point the summary at it. 2. Cut the endpoint from this round's contract and let finance's ask come back through specify properly. 3. Leave it, treating design-disclosure.md's note as sufficient sourcing (not recommended — it's a rung-4 minimalism claim, not a requirements trace).
**Why it matters**: An endpoint with no traceable source and no defined caller is the kind of thing that ships without anyone having decided who's allowed to pull a marina's full reading history as CSV.
