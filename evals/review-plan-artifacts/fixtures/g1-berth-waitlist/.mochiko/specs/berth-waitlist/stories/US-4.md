# US-4 — Marina staff see the queue per berth class (P2)

As marina staff, I want to see who is waiting for each berth class, in order, with dates and
vessel lengths, so that I can plan reshuffles and answer owners who call.

- **Given** entrants on the finger-berth waitlist, **when** staff open the queue, **then** they see each entrant's position, date range, vessel length, and whether an offer is open.

**Independent test:** with three entrants, the queue lists three rows in join order.

Feature: FEAT-031
