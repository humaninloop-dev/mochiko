# Berth-side stories — rewritten by the harbour master, 2026-09-16

- US-1 As the office, I book a visiting boat onto a berth for one or more nights and see it on
  the plan for every night of the stay.
- US-2 As the office, I move a booked boat to another berth when a pontoon goes out of service,
  and the skipper is told the new berth.
- US-3 As a skipper, my confirmation names the berth number and the pontoon, with a map link.
- US-4 As the office, I see tonight's free berths by length and draught, not only by name.
- US-5 As the office, I block a berth for maintenance so nothing can be booked onto it until I
  unblock it.
- US-6 As the harbour master, I see how many berth-nights were booked this month against the
  same month last year.
