# From the harbour master, Port Ellery — 2026-09-15

Three asks before the October opening, and one question.

1. **Longer stays at a per-night rate.** Visiting boats want to book a week at a time. Today the
   office keys seven separate nights and the price is seven times the night rate. We want one
   booking for the whole stay, and the per-night rate should drop after the third night
   (visitor rate for nights 1–3, 15 % off from night 4). The confirmation should say the whole
   stay and its total.

2. **The fuel dock.** The new pump goes in during October. We will sell diesel and petrol at
   the dock; a sale is either paid at the pump or put on the skipper's account, and I need to
   see the tank stock after every delivery and every sale. Nothing in the product does anything
   like this today.

3. **The season-pass discount is three different numbers.** The booking screen says 10 % off,
   the invoice takes 10 % off berth nights but not off electricity, and the reminder email quotes
   the undiscounted total. Pass holders are furious, and it is not one screen — it is the
   booking, the invoice, and the email all disagreeing.

Can all of this go in one go before the opening?
