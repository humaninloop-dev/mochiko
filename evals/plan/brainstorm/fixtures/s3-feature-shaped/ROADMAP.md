# Roadmap — Saltmarsh

**Thesis.** Small sailing schools lose seats and goodwill to spreadsheets and unanswered
emails. Saltmarsh wins by making the boring parts — booking, waitlists, deposits — behave
the same way at every school, with as few knobs as possible.
