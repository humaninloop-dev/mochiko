# Pricing and margins — figures the maintainers use for decisions

Updated 2026-08-28 from the bookings table (2026 season to date) and a nine-school survey
run in August.

## The typical course

- Five-day RYA Day Skipper practical, list price **£595 per seat** (the median across
  schools; the range is £520–£690). Schools set their own prices.
- Saltmarsh takes a **6% platform fee per booking** — £35.70 on a £595 seat.
- Capacity 5 per boat; typical fill **4.2 seats** per course this season.
- Deposits: 30% at booking through Stripe Checkout, balance due 14 days before the start.

## What a course costs a school

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Instructor (five days) | £1,250 |
| Boat, fuel, mooring (£175 × 5) | £875 |
| Consumables and insurance | £80 |
| **Total** | **£2,205** |

Revenue at typical fill (4.2 × £595) is £2,499, leaving **£294 per course — about £70 per
booked seat**. A full boat (5 × £595 = £2,975) leaves £770, about £154 per seat. The fifth
seat is where the money is.

## Discounts today

- No platform-wide discount of any kind.
- Six schools hand-issue a £25 "returning student" coupon through their own Stripe
  dashboard. Saltmarsh does not see or track these.
- Blackwater Sailing has asked twice for "a code I can give a student to bring a friend".

## Where new students come from (survey, nine schools, August 2026)

| Source | Share |
|---|---|
| Word of mouth | 58% |
| Search | 24% |
| RYA training-centre listing | 12% |
| Other | 6% |

## Things that bear on any discount scheme

- Prices are the school's; a platform discount is either funded from the school's price or
  from Saltmarsh's 6% fee, and nothing today says which.
- Stripe Checkout supports promotion codes natively; Saltmarsh does not pass any through.
- The booking audit trail records who changed a booking and when, not why a price differed.
