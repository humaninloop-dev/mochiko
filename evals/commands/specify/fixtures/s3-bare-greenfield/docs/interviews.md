# Member and host interviews — first week

Nine members, the host, the space's owner. Names withheld.

## What members said

- Booking today is a shared spreadsheet; double bookings happen most weeks.
- Most bookings are made within an hour of needing the room; a few are recurring
  (Monday standups).
- "I just want to see if the small room is free in the next hour" — seven of nine.
- Cancelling matters: people book a room "just in case" and forget; the credits should
  come back if they cancel before it starts.
- Nobody wants to log in with a password on their phone; the space already emails them
  their invoice, so a link from that email would do.

## What the host said

- Needs to see today's bookings for all six rooms on one screen at the desk.
- Rooms booked and never used are the biggest complaint from other members; wants to
  free a room fifteen minutes after the slot started if nobody showed.
- Phone booths should not need booking at all until they become contested.

## What the owner said

- Credits are the product: plans differ by credit allowance, and unused credits do not
  roll over.
- Start with booking and the desk view; invoicing stays in the existing tool for now.
