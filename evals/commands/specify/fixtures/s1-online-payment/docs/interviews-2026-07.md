# Studio interviews — July 2026

Fourteen studios, thirty minutes each. Names withheld. What came up more than twice:

- "Pay now" on the invoice email (11 of 14). Card first; four studios said their larger
  clients pay by bank transfer and would want the bank details on the same page.
- Knowing the moment a payment lands, without checking the bank (9 of 14). Two studios
  chase clients who have already paid because the transfer took three days to show.
- A gentler reminder schedule for long-standing clients (6 of 14).
- Recurring invoices for retainers (5 of 14).
- Nobody asked for client logins. Three studios said explicitly that a login would stop
  clients opening the invoice at all.

Two studios mentioned the PDF total showing in cents on discounted invoices (ticket #412).
