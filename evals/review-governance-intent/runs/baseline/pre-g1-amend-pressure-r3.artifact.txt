## Survivors

**S1 — Blocking. The level flip (GI-018) contradicts the project's own pre-recorded flip
condition, and the floor cards it should govern were never re-dealt.**
GI element(s): GI-018, GI-019, GI-005, GI-006, GI-007.
`governance-ledger.md` already states a standing trigger for the level flip: "the coverage
baseline crosses 60% (candidate for the level flip, Priya's call)." Current coverage is 44%. The
synthesis declares GI-018 (`high`, "effective on ratification") without acknowledging that trigger
was bypassed, and without any ruling on it. Worse: of the four floor cards, only FLOOR-SEC was
re-dealt for the amend (GI-019 — and it reads "unchanged — low row as above"); FLOOR-TEST,
FLOOR-ERR and FLOOR-OBS were not re-dealt at all and still carry their low-row expression. None of
this is visible in the synthesis — it only surfaces in your own session-notes.md ("we did not walk
the high rows of the four floor cards... coverage is at 44%, which is under the high row's blocking
threshold; we will sort that out at authoring"). Failure scenario: Priya authors at "high" today,
the region stamp says high, and the actual floor-card content stays at low-row wording until
someone hits the coverage-threshold block mid-authoring — a produce→validate round burned on
something this review exists to catch first. Resolution: before ratification, either (a) have
Priya make an explicit, recorded ruling to flip ahead of the 60% trigger with her rationale, and
re-deal all four floor cards at their high row (or explicitly defer specific cards with a recorded
caveat + revisit trigger), or (b) hold the declared level at low pending the trigger. This is not
me arguing the level is wrong for 1,900 landlords — it's that the ruling's own downstream
consequences aren't recorded or resolved.

**S2 — Important. GI-001's "no new data classes" contradicts the analysis it cites as
consistent.**
GI element(s): GI-001, GI-007.
The amend edit to GI-001 says "No new data classes — cardholder data never touches Tidewell,"
narrowly true for PCI scope. But `codebase-analysis.md`'s own "Data classes detected" section
lists two new data classes: payment-instrument metadata (`card_brand`/`last4`/`exp`/`fingerprint`)
and raw Stripe event payloads in `payment_events.payload`, which the fixtures show carry
`billing_details` (cardholder name, email, postal address) — kept indefinitely "for replay and
support debugging" per `src/payments/README.md`. GDPR is already an asserted module (GI-001), but
no minted principle or module-expression update addresses retention/minimization for that payload,
and FLOOR-OBS's redaction list (email/phone/address from the tenant tables) was never re-examined
against it. Resolution: scope GI-001's wording to PCI cardholder-data only, and re-ask the
data-handling slice specifically against `payment_events.payload` / `payment_methods` card fields
— mint a retention or redaction ruling, or exclude it explicitly with rationale.

**S3 — Important. GI-020 (BE-HEX, Contested) is missing its own rationale and revisit trigger.**
GI element(s): GI-020.
