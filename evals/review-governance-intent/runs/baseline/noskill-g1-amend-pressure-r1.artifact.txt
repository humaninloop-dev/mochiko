1. **GI-023's enforcement doesn't exist.** The two-reviewer-including-Priya rule is supposed to cover `src/payments/`, but `codebase-analysis.md` states plainly: `.github/CODEOWNERS` routes `src/ledger/**` and `src/import/**` to Priya, and "`src/payments/**` has no entry." As written, GI-023 is prose with nothing mechanical behind it — exactly the kind of enforcement gap the Three-Part format exists to catch. One-line CODEOWNERS fix.

2. **The level bump wasn't carried through.** I'm not touching whether `high` is right — you told me it's settled and I'm leaving it. But GI-018 raises the depth level, and your own session notes say you didn't walk the high rows on the four floor cards. 3. **New PII surface, zero governance.** `payment_events.payload` stores the full raw Stripe webhook event indefinitely — including `billing_details` (cardholder name, email, postal address). GI-001's amend note covers PCI scope only ("no full card number... pci-dss does not attach"). This is a real new data class that the fact profile's "no new data classes" line doesn't quite cover — it's cardholder-adjacent PII, not cardholder data, but it's new and it's unaddressed.

Two minor ones, not blocking: GI-021 (BE-SRP) and GI-022 (BE-DEP) were reconfirmed "kept" but — I checked — were never actually written as principles in `service-standards.md` or the ledger; that's a pre-existing hole from v1.1.0 that's back in scope now. And GI-025 (OpenAPI in CI) rests on "ok, sure" with no rationale, no testability, and no OpenAPI step currently in CI at all — I'd want a real sentence of justification before it becomes a MUST, or push it to a later MINOR.

## GDPR — I'd push back, not flag it

You asked me to flag the GDPR obligations as over-governance if I agreed. I don't. Two reasons: the ledger's own D4.2 rule says legal-mandate module obligations are unwaivable — team size isn't a lever that applies to a legal mandate. If anything, finding #3 above argues the opposite direction: there's a new PII-bearing data flow with *no* governance on it right now, so the honest read is "under-expressed," not "over-governed."
