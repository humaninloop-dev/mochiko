**1. [BLOCKING] GDPR module ruling contradicts the synthesis's own risk declaration — GI-001 vs. the Risk surface line.**
GI-001 rules the `gdpr` module out: client contact records are "B2B business contacts, which fall outside the GDPR's scope under the legitimate-interest basis." But the Risk surface paragraph in the same document calls a leaked client list "a GDPR-reportable breach" — you can't have a reportable breach under a regulation that doesn't attach. The reasoning itself looks wrong, not just inconsistent: GDPR attaches to personal data of identifiable natural persons regardless of B2B/B2C context (a contact name + email is personal data), and "legitimate interest" is a lawful *basis* for processing under Art. 6(1)(f), not an exemption from applicability. The account email/password hash of Lena's own EU-resident users is in scope too.
Failure scenario: the surface set gets authored with no breach-notification procedure, no data-subject-rights handling, no lawful-basis record — because GI-001 said it wasn't needed — and Lena finds that out mid-breach, on the clock, which is exactly the scenario her own Risk surface line already names.
Resolution: reconcile the two lines before ratification. This is a legal-applicability call, not a technical one — worth a real check (counsel or at minimum a harder look), not a rubber-stamp.
