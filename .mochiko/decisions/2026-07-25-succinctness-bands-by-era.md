# Succinctness bands by authorship era

**Status:** ruled · **Date:** 2026-07-25 (user-ratified at the strip pass's wave-4 close)
**Context:** the skill-succinctness pass closed with a clean split: every deep cut (35–45%) landed on a pre-doctrine body; the under-band-with-KEPT cohort was precisely the post-doctrine set (authoring-commands 1%, validation-command-shape 1%, loop-discipline ~0, …). Pass record: `.mochiko/brainstorms/skill-succinctness-strip/record.md`; primary evidence: `.mochiko/strips/authoring-commands.md` KEPT entry.

**Decision:** any future succinctness pass sets its band by authorship era — pre-doctrine bodies keep 30–70 never-assessed / 10–40 previously-assessed; post-doctrine skills take 10–40 or an assessment-based expectation. An under-band close with a KEPT entry is the expected healthy outcome for post-doctrine skills. D1 (calibration bar, not quota) unchanged.

**Rationale:** post-doctrine authoring already practices the referencing discipline the strip enforces; a 30–70 expectation misfires on it by construction.

**Alternatives:** uniform band with per-skill judgment (rejected — the pass showed the misfire is systematic, not per-skill).
