# Persona eval pilot 2 (`validator`) — the target is proven for must-shaped judgment claims; dispatch claims are out of instrument for grep-fenced graders

- **Status:** ruled
- **Date:** 2026-09-09
- **Context:** Pilot 2 ran the plan-only persona target on `validator` with a pre-registered
  positive control on the v0.78.0 cheap-rung retarget (`mochiko:explorer` → native `Explore` at
  `model: haiku`) and four goldens (`evals/agents/validator/`; read in the pre-registration fill
  log; evidence `runs/{probe,pilot2-prune,pilot2}`; spend $14.23 of $40). Read: the five
  must-shaped judgment claims — read the file or FAIL, refuse own work, refuse to author, run
  machine-decidable checks first, rank deterministic evidence — held under pass^k in both arms on
  every golden with zero common-claim regressions; seven further claims read model-native at
  the prune (the bare model already defaults FAIL, keeps the verdict binary, writes specific fix
  lists). The control was **not exercised**: the `pre` seat never named `mochiko:explorer`, the
  `post` seat named `Explore` + `haiku` in one of three replicates and otherwise judged 34
  twelve-line stubs cheaper to read than to brief — the golden is grep-shaped and `Grep` is
  inside the fence. The noise guard tripped narrowly (17.9 % vs 12.1 %) on exactly the two
  dispatch-conditional claims. Judge calibration read 0.792 against the 0.80 bar, one pair short,
  with three of five disagreements on one rule: whether a concrete foreclosing step ("Write:
  nothing to disk", an explicit authorship check) embodies a Reject-section standard when
  nothing on the golden tempted the behaviour. Sonnet pairwise chose position 2 in 23 of 24
  calls. The user ruled (2026-09-09): accept the reading as recommended.
- **Decision:**
  1. **The plan-only persona target is proven for must-shaped judgment claims.** Two pilots:
     stable pass^k coverage in both arms, clean regression reads, calibration at or one pair
     under the bar, mechanics green on every assert, cost ≈ $15 per persona pilot.
  2. **Dispatch-conditional claims are out of instrument for grep-fenced personas** — the
     cheap-read trio and any worker-rung claim — partition `out-of-instrument`, reason
     `dispatch-conditional`, alongside pilot 1's `latitude-conditional`. Wave A partitions them
     out at mint, before any golden is authored.
  3. **The foreclosing-step rule is settled:** a Reject-section standard reads `reflected` when
     the plan takes a concrete step that forecloses the rejected behaviour (writes nothing,
     checks authorship, declines an edit), whether or not the golden tempted it; a bare
     restatement ("I would refuse if asked") reads `absent`. Judge prompt and labelling rule
     both carry it from the next mint; pilot 2's 0.792 is recorded as read under the stricter
     lead labelling, not re-keyed after the fact.
  4. **The Sonnet pairwise judge is dropped** from the persona target (opt-in only); the
     command target keeps its own until its next grid re-examines it.
  5. **Rollout shape for wave A** (`devils-advocate`, `tech-lead`): rubric minted and partitioned
     with the two conditional reasons applied up front, three to five goldens, pre-registration
     in the pilot form minus the positive control (no known edit exists), a `post`-only baseline
     grid plus prune to fix the band and coverage; the `pre`/`post` read runs at that persona's
     next edit. ≈ $8 per persona.
- **Rationale:** Both pilots draw the same boundary from different sides: a plan-only
  observable reads *judgment* reliably and reads *dispatch decisions* only when the seat makes
  them, which an opus seat with `Grep` seldom does on fixture-sized workspaces. Spending the
  pre-registered remedies (≈ $12) could clean the calibration figure but not move the control
  or the guard, whose cause is structural. Rejected: **spend the remedies first** (formally
  cleaner, same conclusion); **stop and reopen D2** toward execution for the dispatch class (a
  legitimate future ruling — the record's open question — not this pilot's).
- **Alternatives considered:** keep the control claims in the graded set and read them at
  pass^1 (rejected — post-hoc read change, hides the variance); count the pairwise judge's
  position-2 preference as a plan-quality signal (rejected — it is a position bias).
