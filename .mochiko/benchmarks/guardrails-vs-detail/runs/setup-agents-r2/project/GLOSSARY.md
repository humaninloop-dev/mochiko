# Glossary

The project's domain language. Format: `**<term>** — <definition> *(minted <date>, <source>)*`.

**Contractor** — a Ledgerline user; an independent US tradesperson or freelancer who invoices their own clients *(minted 2026-08-10, setup)*
**Client** — a customer of a contractor, who receives an invoice and pays it *(minted 2026-08-10, setup)*
**Invoice** — a billable record a contractor sends a client, carrying line items, amounts, and a payment status *(minted 2026-08-10, setup)*
