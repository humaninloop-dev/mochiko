# Glossary

> The project's domain language. Format: `**<term>** — <definition> *(minted <date>, <source>)*`.

**Invoice** — a billing document a contractor issues to a client, tracked from issued through paid *(minted 2026-08-10, setup)*
**Payment state** — an invoice's paid/unpaid status, the correctness of which is the product's core promise *(minted 2026-08-10, setup)*
**Manual mark-as-paid** — closing an invoice as paid for check/cash payments Stripe did not collect *(minted 2026-08-10, setup)*
