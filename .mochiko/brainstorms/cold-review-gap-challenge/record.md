# Cold-Review Gap Challenge — Decision Record

**Session:** 2026-08-10 · **Status:** accepted (user, 2026-08-10) · **Command:** `/mochiko:brainstorm` (worktree `worktree-brainstorm-cold-review-gap-challenge`, off main @ f1e8f4b)

## Topic

Does `/mochiko:brainstorm`'s end-stage cold review actually challenge the *angles covered in the session* — i.e., does it reliably identify important gaps (dimensions never visited, framings never questioned) rather than only grading what the record already contains?

## Ground facts

- **F1** — `plugins/mochiko/skills/review-brainstorm/SKILL.md` hunt class 2 is "Missing dimension — what angle (cost, failure mode, actor, timescale) was never visited?" — the gap-finding mandate nominally exists, one line in a five-class table, per-decision framing ("per decision: …").
- **F2** — The reviewer's protocol is record-anchored: cold read of the frozen `record.md`, scenario stress "per decision", hunt classes "per decision", reality-grounding against the record's fact map, fitness checklist over the record. No step directs attention at the topic-space *outside* the record's decisions.
- **F3** — "Never raise a `Contested` decision" carve-out exists (new-angle exception); severity classes Critical/Important/Minor; verdict ready/needs-revision/critical-gaps.
- **F4** — Prior-session evidence (kinako-derived): in one pair run, only 2 of 6 Important findings were found by both lens-split reviewers — reviewer overlap on Important-class findings is low.
- **F5** — User's trigger observation: after cold reviews, the session was never offered a *reopen* — "here are gaps, let's brainstorm them one by one." Survivor routing goes to disposition (fold / repair / user ruling, often batched), never back into the iterative-questioning loop. So even when a missing-angle finding exists, its treatment is a one-line ruling, not exploration.
- **F6** — User's counter-evidence: explicitly instructing "find gaps in my thinking" produced observably better review output (user-pasted example, feature-sizing-and-entry-points-era run): 15 findings including genuine coverage finds (orphaned artifacts never given altitudes; a dual-specification conflict the record carried silently), a clean reserved-to-user vs recommended-batch split, and per-finding options + recommendation on the two reserved items. Detection improves with explicit instruction — the default skill framing, not reviewer capability, is the limiting factor. Even in this better run: batch disposition for 13/15, no reopened questioning.

## Decisions

- **D1** — `Confident` — **Scope: both halves.** The session hardens (a) *detection* — making the reviewer genuinely hunt angles the session never visited, beyond the one-line per-decision hunt class 2 — and (b) *treatment* — giving gap-class survivors a route back into exploration (reopened questioning) instead of dying as one-line batch dispositions. Rationale: the halves compound — weak detection leaves nothing to reopen; batch disposition kills a detected gap without the exploration it deserved; F4's low reviewer overlap suggests detection is genuinely lossy.

- **D2** — `Confident` — **Detection: blind angle-map first.** The reviewer's protocol gains a pre-read phase: from the topic statement (and spawn brief) alone — before opening `record.md` — the reviewer enumerates the angles the topic should have covered (an expected-coverage map). Only then the cold read; the reviewer diffs their map against the record's covered ground, and angles present in the map but absent from the record are coverage findings, first-class alongside the five hunt classes. Rationale: F6 shows explicit gap-framing improves detection, but that reviewer still read the record first and the gaps found were adjacent to recorded content; anchoring is structural, and blind-first is the only sequencing where the angle map cannot inherit the record's frame. The F6 framing line ("find gaps in the thinking, not the record") rides inside the new phase text. Prompt-only fix (A) and staged escalation (C) rejected: A leaves anchoring intact, C spends live runs to learn what F6 + anchoring evidence already say.
  **Amended at review (I1, I2, I7):** coverage findings are carved out of the skill's "concrete failure scenario" evidence bar — a coverage finding's evidence is the map-vs-record diff *plus a materiality argument*: it survives only with a stated case that a plausible decision in the record's scope would differ had the angle been visited. Severity rubric for hypothetical harm: **Critical** = a ruling would likely change · **Important** = a ruling would plausibly change · **Minor** = completeness only. Immaterial angles die at the map-diff and never reach D3 routing; solo reviewers hold their own coverage findings to this bar explicitly (the debate they didn't get). Before promotion, a candidate gap is checked against the record's rejected-roads/rationale — an angle the record shows it saw and dismissed is not a gap.

- **D3** — `Confident` — **Treatment: coverage findings route to reopen by default, user rules per gap.** At disposition, survivors of the coverage class (D2's map-diff findings) are presented by the lead as *candidate questioning topics*, not batch disposition lines; for each gap the user rules: explore now (re-enter the `analysis-iterative` loop on that gap, decisions landing in the same record's D-namespace) / rule inline / defer to an open thread. Non-coverage survivors keep the existing fold/repair/ruling path, batches allowed. Rationale: a coverage gap is definitionally unexplored — a one-line ruling on a never-visited dimension is exactly the F5 failure; severity-keyed routing (B) over-triggers on repair-shaped Important findings (F6's run: 13/15 were folds/amends), and pull-only (C) reproduces the status quo F5 documents. Per-gap ruling keeps session cost user-controlled.

- **D4** — `Confident` — **Reopen-born decisions are graded by the verify pass, one bounded round.** Decisions landed through a D3 reopen extend the existing verify pass's jurisdiction: the verify reviewer checks them for internal consistency with the rest of the record and record-fitness (statement + rationale + mark + owner), quoting evidence — not a fresh full cold read. Contradictions it finds are dispositioned normally; no second reopen fires from the verify pass. Rationale: reopen-born decisions originate from the review's own findings and are explored with the user live, so adversarial pressure already touched their subject once; the verify pass is the existing bounded home for post-review material. Delta cold review (B) re-opens sizing per reopen and can ping-pong; unreviewed-by-design (C) leaves possibly-large new rulings weaker-checked than ordinary folds.
  **Amended at review (I6):** the asymmetry is a ruled trade-off, not silence — reopen-born decisions deliberately accept lighter review (no blind-map coverage hunt) to bound rounds; recursion stops at one level by design.

- **D5** — `Confident` — **In a pair, both reviewers build the blind angle map independently; solo, the one reviewer builds it.** The pre-read map is built by every spawned reviewer from the topic statement alone — the lens split governs the *read phase* depth as today, not the map phase. Coverage findings from both maps flow to the lead's existing cross-set merge; duplicates flag as usual. Rationale: the map costs minutes and no extra spawn; F4's low-overlap evidence argues *for* independent duplication here — two blind maps see different angles and the union is the value. Single-lens ownership (B) halves the net to save nearly nothing; a dedicated third coverage seat (C) grows the roster for what a phase line does.

- **D6** — `Confident` — **Blindness is lead-enforced via two-message dispatch.** The lead spawns each reviewer with the topic statement (and spawn brief) only — no record path; the reviewer returns the angle map as a message; only then does the lead send the record location and the cold read begins. Rationale: anchoring is the entire D2 diagnosis, and an instruction-only fence around the exact failure mode being fixed is the soft floor this repo has already seen breached (setup-v3 team-defect: every surrounding instruction followed except the one that mattered); the map landing in the lead's hands pre-read also lets the merge price coverage findings against a genuinely pre-read map. Chosen over spawn-brief-sequencing-plus-trust (A) at a steelmanned, recommendation-free fork after a flagged adoption streak.

- **D7** — `Confident` — **Map inputs: topic statement + goal line, plus free repo grounding; the session's own artifacts are the only exclusion.** Before mapping, the reviewer may read anything in the repo — CLAUDE.md, ROADMAP, the primitives the topic names — except the session's artifacts (`record.md`, its index entry, anything under the session directory). Rationale: the anchor D2 diagnoses is the *session's* frame; the repo is shared ground the session and reviewer both legitimately stand on, and the F6 run's coverage finds were repo-grounded. Topic-only (A) risks generic maps on thin topics; a lead-written context card (C) is authored by someone who knows the record — a summary-form leak of exactly the channel D6 closed.

- **D8** — `Contested` — **Build scope: both review skills now.** The blind-angle-map phase, coverage-finding class, two-message dispatch, and reopen routing land in the brainstorm cluster (`review-brainstorm` + `brainstorm.md`) *and* in the setup cluster (`review-governance-intent` + `setup.md`'s G3 dispatch and disposition path) in one build. User ruled over the lead's recommendation to ship brainstorm-only and carry setup as an open thread: the lead argued the session's evidence (F5, F6) is all brainstorm-side and setup's synthesis review has different anatomy (synthesis user-confirmed during the session, coverage arbitrated at interrogation) with no observed failure; the user ruled one doctrine, two carriers, built together. Setup-side adaptation details are the builder's to work out within this record's D2–D7 semantics.
  **Rider at review (I4 — new angle, ship-both undisturbed):** `review-governance-intent` already carries a fixed coverage baseline — its hunt class 1 diffs the synthesis against the interrogation agenda's ten dimensions. On the setup side the agenda-diff stays **primary**; the blind map **augments** it, hunting angles outside the ten dimensions; on overlap the agenda governs. The builder lands this reconciliation in the carrier text, not as a second competing mechanism.

- **D9** — `Confident` — **Reliability: no pre-build benchmark; first-live-run watch on both carriers.** (Review fold I5.) This session's own review is the first live dogfood of the mechanism — the blind map was built under D6/D7 dispatch and its map-diff produced the review's principal survivors — so the mechanism ships on that datapoint plus F6, with a first-live-run watch item in BACKLOG covering both carriers instead of a pre-build seeded benchmark. Rationale: the adversarial-review-generality benchmark answered a different question (whether checklists ceiling findings); here the live run already demonstrated the map-diff yields coverage findings a record-anchored read missed, and the residual question — reliability across topics — is exactly what a watch item measures.

- **D10** — `Confident` — **Hunt class 2 narrows to intra-decision scope; topic-level coverage belongs to the new class.** (Review fold I3.) Class 2's text in `review-brainstorm` is re-keyed to *within-decision* missing factors ("this decision never weighed cost/failure mode/actor/timescale"); never-visited *topic* dimensions are the coverage class's jurisdiction via the map-diff. This is a text change to a shipped skill: a supersession-by-ruling strip entry in `.mochiko/strips/review-brainstorm.md` is owed at build, citing this record. Keep-both-unbounded rejected (double-reporting), full supersession rejected (intra-decision misses are real and the map won't catch them).
