## Q3 — the floor (asked after the lead's one push-back on drift evidence)

**"Which invariants stay non-discretionary — beyond the lead's authority to waive? (My recommendation: all four; each is cheap and guards a distinct failure.)"** *(header: The floor, multi-select)*
- User gates — final acceptance and rulings-that-are-yours stay yours; the lead never self-accepts a deliverable.

**Answer: all four selected** → D2.
