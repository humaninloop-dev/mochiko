# Glossary

Terms minted by mochiko's rulings, one line each — the ruling holds the rationale. Format and
invariants: `.mochiko/memory/knowledge-management.md`.

**mode** — what a visitor's success looks like on one surface — Persuade, Operate, Read, or Experience — chosen per surface, never per product, and named in the spec's Direction block *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D3/D5)*
**direction contract** — the surface's settled design intent (thesis · own world · story · first viewport · viewports) carried in the `Contract` field of the Direction block in the spec's Screens & Flows; the build executes it and never re-decides it *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D3/D5)*
**design truth** — the durable, product-wide part of the design baseline at `.mochiko/product/design/`: platform, brand commitments and voice, design principles, the evidence fence, and a pointer to the accessibility standard of record *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D5/D6)*
**design system part** — the evolving part of the design baseline — tokens, type scale, color, spacing, components, motion rules — written only from shipped code at landing, never from intent *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D5/D7)*
**evidence fence** — the design-truth line that forbids inventing claims, testimonials, benchmarks, customers, or pricing where evidence is absent *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D6)*
**craft floor** — the design discipline loaded immediately before any edit on a UX-bearing cycle card: absolute bans, refuse-by-default reflexes, a verify list, executed from the direction contract and never choosing taste itself (`mochiko:patterns-craft-floor`) *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D3/D8)*
**register dial** — the Direction block's `Register` setting — bolder, quieter, distill, or held — that the craft floor executes at the edit *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D9)*
**standard of record** — the one accessibility definition: the `a11y` compliance module where attached, else the Essential Floor line in `mochiko:patterns-code-minimalism`; every other surface points at it and never restates it *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D11)*
