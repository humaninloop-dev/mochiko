# Glossary

Terms minted by mochiko's rulings, one line each — the ruling holds the rationale. Format and
invariants: `.mochiko/memory/knowledge-management.md`.

**mode** — what a visitor's success looks like on one surface — Persuade, Operate, Read, or Experience — chosen per surface, never per product, and named in the spec's Direction block *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D3-no-new-persona/D5-design-baseline-store)*
**direction contract** — the surface's settled design intent (thesis · own world · story · first viewport · viewports) carried in the `Contract` field of the Direction block in the spec's Screens & Flows; the build executes it and never re-decides it *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D3-no-new-persona/D5-design-baseline-store)*
**design truth** — the durable, product-wide part of the design baseline at `.mochiko/product/design/`: platform, brand commitments and voice, design principles, the evidence fence, and a pointer to the accessibility standard of record *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D5-design-baseline-store/D6-product-truth-leg)*
**design system part** — the evolving part of the design baseline — tokens, type scale, color, spacing, components, motion rules — written only from shipped code at landing, never from intent *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D5-design-baseline-store/D7-design-system-landing)*
**evidence fence** — the design-truth line that forbids inventing claims, testimonials, benchmarks, customers, or pricing where evidence is absent *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D6-product-truth-leg)*
**craft floor** — the design discipline loaded immediately before any edit on a UX-bearing cycle card: absolute bans, refuse-by-default reflexes, a verify list, executed from the direction contract and never choosing taste itself (`mochiko:patterns-craft-floor`) *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D3-no-new-persona/D8-implement-design-bites)*
**register dial** — the Direction block's `Register` setting — bolder, quieter, distill, or held — that the craft floor executes at the edit *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D9-other-verbs-fold)*
**standard of record** — the one accessibility definition: the `a11y` compliance module where attached, else the Essential Floor line in `mochiko:patterns-code-minimalism`; every other surface points at it and never restates it *(minted 2026-09-19, `impeccable-design-integration` D11-accessibility-record-standard)*
**joined ID** — a durable numbered ID written as its number then three lowercase topic words, `<ID>-<w1>-<w2>-<w3>` (`GI-004-primitive-audit-ratchet`, `D2-billing-leaves-checkout`), at every single mention including the definition; the number in its owning scope is the key, the slug follows the topic, and a cross-file mention carries its owner (`` `<spec-slug>` FR-012-order-cutoff-time ``); grammar home: `templates/artifact-format.md` IDs section *(minted 2026-10-08, `human-readable-ids` D1-slug-form-reach/D2-joined-form-placement/D11-cross-session-qualifier/D19-slug-grammar-mechanics)*
